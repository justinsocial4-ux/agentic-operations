# Research and Claim Status

Read this file before repeating platform, benchmark, labor, pricing, or outcome claims.

## Current primary documentation

- Salesforce Opportunity History documents dedicated stage-history entries for changes to Stage, Amount, Probability, and Close Date: <https://help.salesforce.com/s/articleView?id=sales.opp_history.htm&language=en_US&type=5>
- Salesforce documents Opportunity History stage-duration reporting and notes that repeated stage visits can be represented differently from accumulated path time: <https://help.salesforce.com/s/articleView?id=000392663&language=en_US&type=1>
- HubSpot documents lifecycle/deal funnel reports and the difference between requiring all selected stages and including any selected stage: <https://knowledge.hubspot.com/reports/create-custom-funnel-reports>
- HubSpot's current funnel builder uses lifecycle or deal stages backed by automatically updated stage-entry timestamps: <https://knowledge.hubspot.com/reports/create-new-custom-funnel-reports>

These sources support stage-history and funnel-report mechanics. They do not establish universal stage orders, dwell targets, minimum sample sizes, severity bands, confidence scores, causal diagnoses, or revenue impact.

## Legacy claims excluded

Do not present the old 50-record minimum, 70% confidence threshold, 3/5/10-point bottleneck bands, 7/14/45-day dwell targets, 25% stuck threshold, 85–95% confidence labels, tool pricing, labor savings, conversion lift, or lost-revenue multiplication as decision facts. The historical research failed its own QA and included citation and assumption defects.

Use customer-approved policies and comparable customer history. Keep external context source-labeled and separate from deterministic decisions.
