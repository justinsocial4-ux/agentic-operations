# Bottleneck Evidence

Read this file before labeling bottlenecks, stuck records, root causes, or revenue impact.

## Evidence ladder

1. `OBSERVATION_ONLY` — point estimates, intervals, and age distributions; no approved comparison rule.
2. `REVIEW_CANDIDATE` — an approved target/baseline rule triggered and data coverage passed.
3. `INVESTIGATION_SUPPORTED` — record sampling or process evidence supports a specific operational hypothesis.
4. `CAUSE_SUPPORTED` — a controlled design or unusually strong causal evidence supports the claimed cause.

Do not skip levels.

## Approved comparison required

A low rate is not automatically a bottleneck. Require a customer target or comparable historical baseline plus a decision rule approved before inspecting the evaluated cohort. Report exact counts, maturity, uncertainty, and missingness.

## Stuck-record language

Use `AGING_RECORD` until an approved stage-age target is exceeded. A universal 7/14/45-day table is not evidence. Current age is not completed dwell.

## Root-cause limits

Segment, source, rep, stage, amount, and activity differences generate hypotheses. They do not prove lead quality, rep behavior, legal delay, qualification misalignment, seasonality, or buyer intent.

## Financial language

- Sum of open amounts = pipeline exposure, not delayed revenue.
- Lost count × average deal size = a scenario, not observed loss.
- Expected value requires an approved probability source, currency handling, and explicit assumptions.
- Never promise lift from a recommendation.

Recommend sample review, policy review, or a controlled change test. Do not auto-change staffing, stage definitions, scoring, or routing.
