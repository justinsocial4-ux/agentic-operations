# Scope and governance

- Purpose is exactly `pseudonymous_pipeline_observation_rule_evidence_review`.
- The packet is customer-supplied, frozen, offline, read-only, and pseudonymous.
- Require effective operations, security, privacy, and legal review receipts; collection, recipient, retention, rule-policy, and review-authorization receipts; and an authorized actual human reviewer.
- Reject credentials, owner identities, URLs, query/error text, free text, pipeline/table/field names, raw logs, record data, and customer content.
- A matched receipt proves evidence alignment only. It does not approve a platform state, incident, cause, impact, or action.
- Humans retain every interpretation and action decision.
