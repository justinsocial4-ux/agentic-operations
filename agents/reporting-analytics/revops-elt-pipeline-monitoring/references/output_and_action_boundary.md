# Output and action boundary

Allowed output:

- exact review, policy, lane, platform, source, schema, definition, rule, and observation IDs;
- exact evidence and customer-rule condition states;
- one supplied value and one supplied threshold in their assigned receipts;
- exact lineage and receipt IDs;
- fixed false action flags.

Forbidden output:

- health, confidence, severity, reliability, risk, compliance, outage, incident, freshness, corruption, priority, or impact labels;
- stack scores, cross-platform aggregation, trends, forecasts, causes, revenue claims, or recommended actions;
- live queries, threshold relaxation, alerts, retries, sync/test triggers, remediation, schedules, messages, writes, publications, or downstream decisions.

No output is an approval or recommendation.
