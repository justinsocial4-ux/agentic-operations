# Verification Sources

These official sources support the boundaries; they do not certify this skill or make customer use lawful.

## Employment and AI governance

- [EEOC and DOJ warning on AI employment tools](https://www.eeoc.gov/newsroom/us-eeoc-and-us-department-justice-warn-against-disability-discrimination) — software used to monitor performance or determine pay and promotion can create disability-discrimination risk.
- [EEOC employment discrimination and AI for workers](https://www.eeoc.gov/sites/default/files/2024-04/20240429_Employment%20Discrimination%20and%20AI%20for%20Workers.pdf) — covers task monitoring and systems influencing training, pay, layoffs, or termination.
- [NIST AI RMF Core](https://airc.nist.gov/airmf-resources/airmf/5-sec-core/) — requires context, construct validation, representative evaluation, uncertainty, human oversight, impacts, safe failure, and feedback/appeal processes.
- [EU AI Act official text](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/1689/oj?locale=en) — identifies AI used to monitor or evaluate workers and affect work relationships as a high-risk employment use case.
- [European Commission AI Act timeline](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/guidelines-ai-high-risk-systems) — current Commission material places employment high-risk rule application on 2 December 2027.

## Worker privacy

- [ICO data protection and monitoring workers](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/employment/monitoring-workers/data-protection-and-monitoring-workers/) — addresses purpose, necessity, transparency, minimization, accuracy, challenge, retention, security, impact assessment, and automated worker monitoring.

## Platform semantics

- [Salesforce record and object metadata](https://developer.salesforce.com/docs/atlas.en-us.uiapi.meta/uiapi/ui_api_resources_record.htm) — object and field metadata are discovered for the authenticated organization rather than assumed universally.
- [HubSpot CRM object model](https://developers.hubspot.com/docs/api-reference/latest/crm/understanding-the-crm) — calls, emails, meetings, owners, records, properties, schemas, and associations have distinct object semantics.
- [HubSpot calls](https://developers.hubspot.com/docs/api-reference/latest/crm/activities/calls/guide), [emails](https://developers.hubspot.com/docs/api-reference/latest/crm/activities/emails/guide), and [meetings](https://developers.hubspot.com/docs/api-reference/latest/crm/activities/meetings/guide) — source timestamps, status, owner, and association meanings differ by activity type.
