# Privacy and Workforce Boundary

Worker monitoring and performance evaluation can affect pay, promotion, discipline, termination, livelihood, privacy, and protected groups. Read-only access does not remove those risks.

## Required boundary

- Process anonymous aggregates only.
- Require the customer's documented workforce/privacy approval before review.
- Require a defined purpose, authorized recipients, retention, correction, and prohibited-use rules.
- Suppress small cohorts with the customer's exact numeric rule.
- Provide no person-level output, re-identification path, free text, recording, or sensitive inference.
- Provide no ranking, score, diagnosis, coaching, investigation, monitoring, quota, territory, compensation, promotion, discipline, termination, hiring, scheduling, leave, or customer action.
- Never claim legal compliance. Jurisdiction, policy, notice, consultation, lawful basis, impact assessment, and human-process obligations remain customer-owned.

## Human review is a boundary, not a cure

`HUMAN_REVIEW_REQUIRED` means the descriptive receipt needs authorized contextual review. It does not permit a human to use this output as a shortcut for a prohibited decision. The helper always returns `action_authorized: false`.
