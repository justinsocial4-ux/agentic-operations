# Scope and Policy

## Required policy receipt

Require one policy that is effective at the UTC cutoff and contains stable IDs for the policy, version, approving authority, and human reviewer. It must define:

- one descriptive aggregate-review purpose plus workforce, privacy, and worker-notice receipts;
- the exact allowed metric codes and aggregation-rule IDs;
- the exact approved cohort dimension;
- a customer-owned minimum-group integer;
- authorized recipients and a retention rule;
- source, schema, authorization, cutoff, and correction requirements;
- prohibited employment, compensation, customer, CRM, messaging, ranking, coaching, and monitoring uses.

Reject expired, future, duplicate, incomplete, or conflicting policy evidence. Configuration is not proof of validity: the customer remains responsible for lawful purpose, notice, workforce consultation, privacy review, and jurisdiction-specific obligations.

## Narrow purpose

The purpose is to review anonymous aggregate evidence exactly as supplied. It is not to evaluate a person, identify a cause, set a target, predict an outcome, or authorize action.

## Freeze and correction

Freeze the policy, normalized input, helper output, and review ID together. A corrected cell, source, cohort, period, or rule creates a new review; never edit a prior receipt in place.
