# Weekly Revenue Narrative Template

Read this when drafting and validating the final report. Keep the main narrative concise; put detailed receipts below it or in an attachment.

```markdown
# Weekly Revenue Narrative — [COMPLETED WEEK]

**Scope:** [CRM] · [PIPELINE/FILTERS] · [TIMEZONE] · [CURRENCY/AMOUNT MEANING]
**Evidence status:** [GO / DEGRADED]

## What Changed
[Two to four factual sentences. State closed-won results, open pipeline, and reliable week-over-week changes.]

## Closed Outcomes
- Closed won: [COUNT] · [AMOUNT BY CURRENCY]
- Closed lost: [COUNT] · [AMOUNT BY CURRENCY]
- Win rate: [RATE or UNKNOWN]
- Versus prior week: [DELTA or explicit non-percentage state]

## Open Pipeline
- Total: [COUNT] · [AMOUNT BY CURRENCY]
- Material stage/owner changes: [SOURCE-TRACEABLE SUMMARY]

## Deals Needing Review
| Deal | Amount | Stage | Evidence | Rules-based result | Proposed human follow-up |
|---|---:|---|---|---|---|

## What Is Unknown
- [Missing snapshot, stage history, activity, currency conversion, or mapping limitation]

## This Week's Focus
1. [Highest-value evidence-backed action]
2. [Second action]
3. [Data repair if needed]

---
Generated [TIMESTAMP] · Validation [PASS/FAIL] · Delivery [PREVIEW/SENT/FAILED]
```

## Writing rules

- Say “closed won” and “closed lost,” not “new ARR” or “churn,” unless contract data proves those meanings.
- Say “rules-based risk flag,” not “likely to lose,” unless a validated predictive model exists.
- Use direct numbers and short sentences.
- Separate facts, heuristics, and unknowns.
- Never claim delivery succeeded without a delivery receipt.
