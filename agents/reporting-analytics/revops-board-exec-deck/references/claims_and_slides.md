# Claims and slides

The approved slide blueprint lists exact slide IDs and allowed metric, comparison, and claim slots. A claim ledger entry carries only a customer-authored claim ID, template ID, evidence IDs, approval receipt, and helper-derived evidence state; the helper does not accept, create, rewrite, or display claim prose. Missing referenced evidence blocks the claim state.

Slide blocks are evidence assembly receipts, not a presentation. Do not invent titles, charts, narrative, speaker notes, visual emphasis, causes, forecasts, or recommendations, and do not reuse prior-deck wording without exact current approval.
