# Metric contracts

Treat each metric label as a customer-defined construct. Require exact unit, currency where applicable, amount basis, definition ID, aggregation method, source/schema/query/page/population receipts, authorization window, reporting period/timezone, freshness, and finance or metric-owner approval.

Do not equate CRM amount with ARR, bookings, forecast, recognized revenue, cash, or GAAP revenue. A non-GAAP measure also requires an exact non-GAAP label ID, directly comparable GAAP contract, reconciliation receipt, period-consistency receipt, and finance approval.
