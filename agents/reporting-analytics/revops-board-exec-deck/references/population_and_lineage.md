# Population and lineage

Every metric contract declares the complete record-ID population and a pseudonymization receipt. Submitted records must match it exactly once and must bind to the same contract, source version, schema version, authorization, query, page, period, freshness, and definition receipts.

Reject duplicates, extras, omissions, stale or future evidence, mixed schemas, partial pages, cached substitutions, silent deduplication, and free text. Preserve missing, conflicting, suppressed, and unauthorized states without numeric substitution.
