- ICO purpose limitation and data minimization for agentic AI: https://ico.org.uk/about-the-ico/research-reports-impact-and-evaluation/research-and-reports/technology-and-innovation/tech-horizons-and-ico-tech-futures/ico-tech-futures-agentic-ai/data-protection-and-privacy-risks/
- ICO profiling and automated-decision guidance: https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/individual-rights/individual-rights/rights-related-to-automated-decision-making-including-profiling/

These sources show that attribution models assign credit according to configured rules or algorithms; platform populations, model availability, sampling, and eligible interactions vary; causal/data-driven claims require far more than campaign membership; and valid measurement plus privacy governance must be explicit. They do not authorize a customer model, causal claim, or decision.
