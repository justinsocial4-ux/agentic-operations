# Allocation math

For each complete opportunity, validate finite non-negative Decimal amount and raw weights, require a positive weight total, normalize the weights, and allocate the approved measure. Quantize to six decimal places and assign the rounding residual to the lexicographically first touchpoint only when the exact remainder-rule receipt authorizes it.

Prove per-opportunity conservation mechanically, then aggregate credit by anonymous bucket within one exact model/period/currency/amount basis. Unresolved opportunities produce no credit. Bucket values are policy-allocated credit, not revenue, ROI, effectiveness, incrementality, or causal contribution.
