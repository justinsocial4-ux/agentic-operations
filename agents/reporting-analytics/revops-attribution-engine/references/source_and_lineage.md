# Source and lineage

Bind one allocation contract to exact source and schema versions, authorization window, query and page receipts, complete opportunity population, cutoff, reporting period, touchpoint window, IANA timezone and matching offsets, currency, amount basis, approved measure definition, finance approval, and pseudonymization receipt.

Reject duplicate, extra, missing, stale, future, partial, cached, unauthorized, suppressed, or schema-conflicting records. Every opportunity and touchpoint must carry its own source and population receipts.
