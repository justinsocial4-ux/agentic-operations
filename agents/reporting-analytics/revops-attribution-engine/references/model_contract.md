# Model contract

Require a stable customer-authored model ID, version, definition receipt, approval receipt, and a separate approved raw-weight receipt for every declared touchpoint. The helper accepts the exact weights; it does not derive them from a model label.

First-touch, last-touch, linear, time-decay, U-shaped, W-shaped, data-driven, and custom labels are policy names, not evidence of causal truth. Never choose a default, tune a parameter, resolve role overlap, compare models, or convert missing weights into a generic rule.
