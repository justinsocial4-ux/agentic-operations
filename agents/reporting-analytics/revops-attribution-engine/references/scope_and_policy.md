# Scope and policy

Require one active customer policy for pseudonymous attribution-credit allocation evidence review. Bind purpose, privacy and lawful-basis review, reviewer role, allowed recipient roles, retention, and explicit prohibitions on causal claims, profiling, ranking, ROI, optimization, budget, forecast, messaging, export, writes, and downstream automated decisions.

System access, a platform attribution setting, a prior report, or a job title does not grant authority. The helper never chooses a model or authorizes interpretation or action.
