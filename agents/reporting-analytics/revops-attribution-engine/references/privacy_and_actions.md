# Privacy and actions

Reject names, emails, phones, URLs, UTM strings, direct CRM/advertising IDs, account/contact/deal identifiers, free text, and reversible pseudonyms. Require privacy, lawful-basis, pseudonymization, recipient, and retention receipts even though the helper receives anonymous IDs.

Never profile people or organizations, rank buckets, recommend spend, alter bids or campaigns, influence forecasts, send alerts/messages, export data, schedule runs, write records, or feed a consequential downstream decision. Human reviewers own model selection, interpretation, validation, and action.
