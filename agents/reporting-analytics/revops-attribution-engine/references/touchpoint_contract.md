# Touchpoint contract

Use anonymous opportunity, touchpoint, and bucket IDs only. Require exact receipts for opportunity-to-touchpoint identity linkage, observed-event definition, eligibility, deduplication, sequence ordering, exclusions, and bucket mapping. Event timestamps must fall inside the approved touchpoint window and follow one contiguous sequence per opportunity.

Campaign membership, CreatedDate, LastModifiedDate, status, UTM values, names, email, phone, or account association does not independently prove an interaction or an outcome relationship. Never infer Direct Sales, Other, engagement, identity, or a missing touchpoint.
