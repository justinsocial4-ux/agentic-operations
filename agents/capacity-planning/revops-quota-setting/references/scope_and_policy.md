# Scope and Policy

The only allowed purpose is `quota_policy_scenario_review`: calculate transparent arithmetic over a complete anonymous customer-authored plan. The skill does not decide whether a quota is fair, achievable, legal, strategic, or appropriate.

Require a stable policy ID/version, owner role, human reviewer role, UTC effective and cutoff times, optional UTC expiry, IANA timezone, correction and appeal paths, privacy/workforce/compensation review receipts, affected-worker notice receipt, exact calculation-rule approvals, source bindings, and every prohibition enforced by the helper. Silence is not approval.

Reject an expired, future, mismatched, or incomplete policy or authorization. A correction creates a new frozen review version; it never overwrites evidence.
