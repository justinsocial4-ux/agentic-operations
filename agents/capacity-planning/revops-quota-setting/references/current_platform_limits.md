# Current Platform Limits

HubSpot deal records are property-based. Requested undefined properties can be absent and defined-but-empty properties can be null. Customer schemas, pipelines, currencies, permissions, and meanings vary, so no portable quota or coverage field is assumed.

Salesforce forecasting access and values depend on permissions, forecast roles, configuration, category mappings, adjustments, currency, and object metadata. Standard-looking fields do not establish a quota policy or a comparable coverage basis.

This skill connects to neither platform. Accept only an approved anonymous export with frozen schema, meanings, population, timestamps, bases, and authorization. Never accept owner names, individual opportunity history, worker performance, or compensation records.
