# Platform and research limits

Verify live customer configuration and current official documentation before making platform claims. Salesforce territory models, ownership, teams, associations, assignment rules, and sharing behavior differ by edition/configuration. Google Routes matrix requests have element limits, per-element statuses/fallbacks, policy/attribution/caching duties, and pricing that can change: https://developers.google.com/maps/documentation/routes/usage-and-billing and https://developers.google.com/maps/documentation/routes/policies .

Do not repeat inherited claims about universal territory counts, account minimums, balance bands, travel savings, quota uplift, attrition reduction, solver runtime, API limits, pricing, or production readiness. Vendor pages and case studies may describe products; they do not validate this customer's model or causal outcomes.

Record what was verified, the date, source URL/version, and remaining uncertainty. Route, solver, CRM, workforce, privacy, accessibility, labor, and legal evidence each needs its own qualified owner. This skill does not issue legal advice or certify compliance.
