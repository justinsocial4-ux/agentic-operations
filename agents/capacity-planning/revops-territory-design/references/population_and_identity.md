# Population and identity

Require complete frozen account and rep populations with stable pseudonymous IDs. Record current assignment, territory/team/overlay role, inclusion state, effective time, source, and correction owner separately.

Every in-scope account must appear exactly once in each candidate. Multiple reps are permitted only under an approved shared/team model that defines roles and counting treatment. Preserve missing, duplicate, unassigned, unknown, excluded, pinned, overlay, and relationship records as explicit states.

Use approved work-anchor IDs for route evidence. Do not ingest names, emails, home addresses or coordinates, protected traits, manager narratives, content, historical quota attainment, discipline, or performance by default. Leave, ramp, accommodation, accessibility, labor-rule, and identity conflicts go to `IDENTITY_REVIEW`; they do not become zero capacity or neutral values.
