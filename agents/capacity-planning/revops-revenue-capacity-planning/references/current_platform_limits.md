# Current Platform Limits

## HubSpot

HubSpot records are property-based and accounts can define custom properties. Current APIs can omit requested properties that are absent, and sensitive properties use separate scopes. A deal `amount` or a custom ARR field therefore has no portable capacity meaning without a customer-specific schema, basis, currency, completeness, and authorization receipt.

## Salesforce

Salesforce exposes object metadata and fields such as Opportunity amount, stage, and close date, but organizations customize objects, stages, validation, currency, and access. Those fields do not prove an anonymous capacity-unit basis or justify employee-level analysis.

## Safe mapping

This skill does not connect to either platform. A customer may supply an approved anonymous aggregate export only after freezing the platform schema/version, field meanings, population, observation time, capture time, and authorization. Never accept owner names, titles, individual opportunity performance, activity, quota, or employment history.
