# Action Boundaries

## Never authorized

Do not recommend or initiate hiring, firing, replacement, recruiting, promotion, compensation, quota, territory, account allocation, workload, schedule, budget, forecast, pricing, customer, or CRM action.

Do not rank scenarios, call one better, label a gap large or small, call current capacity sufficient or insufficient, or state that a target is achievable. No numeric adequacy adjective is defined.

Do not create alerts, tasks, tickets, messages, dashboards, recurring checks, or downstream handoffs. Do not schedule a rerun. Do not write output into another system.

## Human boundary

The result is arithmetic evidence for a named human-review process outside the skill. That process must consider legal, finance, HR, strategy, fairness, workload, customer, and operational context before any decision. The helper always states that action is not authorized.
