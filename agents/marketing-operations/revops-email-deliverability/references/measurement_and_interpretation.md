# Measurement and interpretation

- Complaint ratios depend on the provider's numerator and denominator.
- Bounce, delivery error, rejection, temporary failure, and inbox placement are not interchangeable.
- Google states Postmaster data is not real time, may be incomplete at low volume, and that its user-reported spam ratio can be inexact.
- Google does not track open rates and cannot verify third-party open-rate accuracy.
- Reputation is one factor among many and is not a universal numeric score.
- Correlation among authentication, complaints, placement, content, or volume does not establish root cause.
- Missing data is not zero; a missing listing response is not `not-listed`; stale data is not current evidence.
- Industry benchmarks and commercial case studies cannot replace the customer's source-specific definition or population.
