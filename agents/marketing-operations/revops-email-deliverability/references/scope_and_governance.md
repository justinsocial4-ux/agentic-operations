# Scope and governance

- Purpose is exactly `pseudonymous_email_deliverability_observation_evidence_review`.
- The packet is customer-supplied, frozen, offline, read-only, and pseudonymous.
- Require effective operations, security, privacy, and legal review receipts; collection, recipient, retention, and review-authorization receipts; and an authorized actual human reviewer.
- Reject credentials, domains, IPs, URLs, names, addresses, message content, subject lines, headers, free text, and recipient-level data.
- A matched receipt shows evidence alignment only. It does not approve a configuration, sender, campaign, legal position, or action.
- Humans retain every interpretation and action decision.
