# Current Platform Limits

Google Ads exposes multiple conversion measures and lets reports segment by conversion action; conversion actions carry category, counting, attribution, and lookback settings. Conversion data may be delayed, and zero-metric rows may be omitted.

LinkedIn reporting varies by pivot and requested fields, has result and retention constraints, may return empty data for no activity or no access, and reports some measures approximately. Totals can differ across reporting levels or summed daily ranges. HubSpot campaign properties are open-ended, and asset metrics require an explicit date range. Never assume one universal platform schema or equivalent conversion meaning.
