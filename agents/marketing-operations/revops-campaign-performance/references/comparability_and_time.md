# Comparability And Time

Contracts may share a measurement group only when reporting level, local period boundaries, IANA timezone, currency, amount basis, all metric-definition IDs, conversion action category, counting mode, attribution model, attribution window, and date basis match exactly. Require a customer-approved equivalence receipt even when those fields match.

Do not convert account-local dates to another reporting day, apply FX, compare incomplete periods, or add daily rows to a platform total unless the supplied contract explicitly defines that basis. Different sources alone never prove comparability.
