# Scope And Policy

Use only for the approved purpose `pseudonymous_campaign_measurement_evidence_review`. Require effective policy, source, measurement, equivalence, recipient, and retention receipts plus named owner and human-reviewer roles. Reject any request whose purpose includes targeting, optimization, ranking, attribution, forecasting, budget allocation, messaging, or system action.

The output is evidence for a human review. It is not approval, a decision, a platform reconciliation, or a performance verdict.
