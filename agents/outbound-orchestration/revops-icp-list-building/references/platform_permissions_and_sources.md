# Platform permissions and sources

Read this before interpreting CRM or vendor evidence. Salesforce object and field access depends on the context user's object- and field-level permissions. HubSpot separately controls record view/edit, enrichment, export approval, segment editing, scoring, marketing publishing, and workflow enrollment. A record visible in one context is not proof that another field, export, enrichment, or action is authorized.

Accept only a frozen customer-supplied pseudonymous observation with exact source-authorization and capture receipts. Never query Salesforce, HubSpot, a warehouse, an enrichment vendor, a social network, or a public source. Never treat vendor presence, an activity event, a title, a company attribute, or a historical outcome as fit, intent, readiness, or permission.
