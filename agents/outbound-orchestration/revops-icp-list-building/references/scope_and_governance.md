# Scope and governance

Read this before accepting a review. The only allowed purpose is `pseudonymous_candidate_rule_evidence_review`. Require an effective policy, cutoff, IANA timezone, exact human reviewer, allowed recipient, and unique operations, security, privacy, legal, collection, lawful-basis-review, notice, objection, suppression, correction/access, recipient, retention, pseudonymization, rule-policy, population-policy, and review-authorization receipts.

The policy must prohibit identity data, contact data, credentials, live access, enrichment, fuzzy inference, proxy inference, scoring, ranking, prioritization, threshold relaxation, export, CRM writes, outreach, notifications, retries, scheduling, publication, and downstream decisions. A reviewer may inspect the evidence receipt only; the review does not approve any later action.
