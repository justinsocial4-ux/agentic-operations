# Candidate and population contract

Read this before accepting candidate rows. Require one nonempty declared population of stable pseudonymous `candidate-*` IDs, one packet receipt, one population receipt, one completeness receipt, and one pseudonymization receipt. The candidate rows must equal the declared population exactly, and every rule must declare that same population.

Reject direct or reversible identity fields, company names, person names, domains, email addresses, phone numbers, postal addresses, social profiles, free text, job titles, industries, technology names, behavior, message content, notes, and raw CRM or vendor records. A pseudonymous code is still governed data; it is not permission to resolve identity or contact anyone.
