# Privacy, direct marketing, and identity

Read this before handling any prospecting request. The review requires customer-supplied receipts for collection, lawful-basis review, notice, objection, suppression, correction/access, recipients, retention, pseudonymization, and human review. These receipts prove only that the customer supplied an approved artifact; the helper does not declare legal compliance.

Never accept or reveal direct identity or contact details. Never enrich a person or company, combine third-party or public data into a profile, infer intent, or convert a rule condition into permission to market. Suppression and objection evidence fails closed. Human legal and privacy owners control every later identity, marketing, sharing, or contact decision.
