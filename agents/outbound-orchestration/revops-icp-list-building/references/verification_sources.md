# Current official verification sources

Read this when a platform, privacy, profiling, or reliability claim appears.

- UK ICO, Direct marketing guidance, updated 28 April 2026: data-protection-by-design, lawful basis, fair and clear collection/use explanation, preference controls, and the absolute right to object. https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/direct-marketing-and-privacy-and-electronic-communications/direct-marketing-guidance/
- UK ICO, Plan direct marketing: profiling and third-party/public-source expansion require transparency, accuracy, minimization, risk consideration, and respect for objections; lawful-basis analysis is contextual. https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/direct-marketing-and-privacy-and-electronic-communications/direct-marketing-guidance/plan-direct-marketing/
- Salesforce Developers, Query Objects: the context user's object- and field-level permissions control accessible objects and fields. https://developer.salesforce.com/docs/platform/graphql/guide/query-record-objects.html
- HubSpot, User permissions guide, updated 6 March 2026: record access, export approval, enrichment, segments, scoring, marketing publishing, and workflow enrollment are separately permissioned. https://knowledge.hubspot.com/user-management/hubspot-user-permissions-guide
- NIST AI RMF Core: define intended use, data suitability and representativeness, construct validation, limits, human oversight, third-party risks, deployment-like evaluation, and safe failure. https://airc.nist.gov/airmf-resources/airmf/5-sec-core/

These sources support conservative boundaries. They do not prove that any supplied customer policy, source, rule, candidate, or marketing use is lawful, accurate, valid, or effective.
