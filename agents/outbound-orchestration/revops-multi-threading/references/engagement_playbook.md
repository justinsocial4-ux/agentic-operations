# Human-Approved Introduction Playbook

Read this before proposing stakeholder engagement.

1. Confirm the function genuinely matters for the deal stage.
2. Ask a verified stakeholder who owns that function and why.
3. Request a warm introduction when possible.
4. State a role-relevant meeting purpose without inventing personalization.
5. Respect opt-outs, permissions, and contact restrictions.
6. Ask the rep to approve the person, message, channel, and timing.
7. After interaction, update the role evidence through the normal CRM workflow and record the verifier.

Examples of purposes:

- financial approval: validate business case and approval process;
- technical review: confirm requirements, integration, security, and acceptance criteria;
- procurement/legal review: clarify process, terms, and timing;
- end-user review: validate workflow and adoption needs.

Do not scrape, enrich, send, or create tasks as part of analysis unless separately authorized.
