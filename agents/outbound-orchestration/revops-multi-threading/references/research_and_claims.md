# Research and Claim Status

Read this before repeating multi-threading outcome claims.

Current official product documentation confirms that Salesforce Opportunity Contact Roles and HubSpot Buying role properties can store deal-role evidence. It does not prove that a title determines authority or that a contact count causes a specific win rate.

The old skill's 5% versus 30% win rates, 6x improvement, $300k–$2M recovered revenue, 1–3 hours saved per account, 80–95% role accuracy, and automatic risk scores were not safe as universal decision facts. They are removed from the operating logic.

Measure customer-specific coverage, role verification accuracy, introduction acceptance, and outcomes. Use matched or experimental analysis before claiming causal business impact.
