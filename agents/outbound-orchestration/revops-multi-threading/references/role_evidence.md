# Buying Role Evidence

Read this before assigning or displaying a role.

## Evidence levels

### VERIFIED

Use when an opportunity/deal role was explicitly set by an authorized user, confirmed in a deal review, or supplied by an approved system of record. Retain who verified it and when.

### INFERRED_CANDIDATE

Use when title, department, seniority, hierarchy, or activity suggests a possible role. This is a search/ranking aid, not authority proof.

### UNKNOWN

Use when evidence is absent, stale, or conflicting.

## Role principles

- A Champion demonstrates internal advocacy; title cannot prove it.
- Economic Buyer and Budget Holder depend on actual authority for this purchase, not generic seniority.
- Technical Buyer depends on evaluation responsibility for this deal.
- End User depends on intended use.
- Legal, Security, Compliance, and Procurement are review functions. Do not label a person a blocker based on department.
- One person may fill multiple roles; one role may have multiple people.

Never turn a heuristic score into “90% certain this is the buyer.”
