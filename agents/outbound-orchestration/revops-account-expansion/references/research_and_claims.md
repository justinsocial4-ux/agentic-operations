# Research and Claims

Checked 2026-07-10. Platform documentation supports data structures and configuration—not a universal expansion model.

## Primary platform sources

- [Amplitude: Plan your account instrumentation](https://amplitude.com/docs/analytics/plan-your-accounts-instrumentation) documents group/account analysis and warns that group/property updates are forward-looking rather than retroactive.
- [Amplitude: Create a tracking plan](https://amplitude.com/docs/data/create-tracking-plan) documents governed event/property definitions.
- [Amplitude: Taxonomy API](https://amplitude.com/docs/apis/analytics/taxonomy) documents programmatic access to event/property taxonomy.
- [Amplitude: Salesforce Group source](https://amplitude.com/docs/data/source-catalog/salesforce-group) documents mapping Salesforce account rows into Amplitude groups and scheduled synchronization.
- [Salesforce: Set up Opportunity Products](https://help.salesforce.com/s/articleView?id=sales.opp_setup.htm&language=en_US) documents product use on opportunities.
- [HubSpot: Default deal properties](https://knowledge.hubspot.com/properties/hubspots-default-deal-properties) documents Amount, company currency, ACV, ARR, MRR, and TCV property meanings.
- [HubSpot: Create subscriptions](https://knowledge.hubspot.com/subscriptions/create-subscriptions) documents recurring subscription records and associations.

## Claim boundaries

These sources do not establish the legacy skill's universal account minimum, ARR floor, data-coverage thresholds, freshness/maturity rules, seat buffer, play tree, propensity weights, tier bands, renewal window, value multipliers, probability language, or promised expansion outcomes.

Do not present vendor documentation as independent evidence of customer behavior. Validate definitions, thresholds, scores, and plays against the customer's own data and decision policy before operational use.
