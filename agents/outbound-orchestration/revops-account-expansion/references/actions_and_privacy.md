# Actions, Contacts, and Privacy

## Read-only default

The analysis may prepare a proposed action, but it must not create or edit CRM records, opportunities, tasks, sequences, contact roles, messages, or meetings.

## Contact evidence

Use only the minimum contact data needed for the approved action. Record source, freshness, relationship evidence, consent/legal basis where applicable, and access restrictions. A title can suggest a candidate for human verification; it does not prove budget authority, product ownership, or willingness to engage.

## Approval receipt

Every proposed action should show:

- account and approved owner
- candidate/play label and supporting evidence
- blockers and unknowns
- proposed contact-selection method
- proposed channel and purpose
- exact system mutation or message preview
- named approver and approval status

Keep the status `PREVIEW_ONLY` until the user separately approves the exact action. Analysis approval is not execution approval.

## Safety

Do not expose support, payment, employee, or user-level product data beyond the approved audience. Aggregate when account-level evidence is sufficient. Preserve opt-outs, suppression rules, and territory/owner constraints.
