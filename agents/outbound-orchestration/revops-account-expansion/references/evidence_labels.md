# Evidence Labels

## `VERIFIED_SIGNAL`

Use only for a direct, dated fact from a named source with resolved identity and sufficient coverage. Examples: contracted 100 seats, 90 approved active users in a named window, renewal date from the active contract, or a current verified unit price.

## `INFERRED_CANDIDATE`

Use for a policy-based hypothesis assembled from verified signals. State the policy rule and every missing limitation. This label does not mean intent, likelihood, propensity, probability, demand, or promised conversion.

## `UNKNOWN`

Use when required evidence is absent, outside the observation window, stale under policy, or not instrumented. Unknown is not zero and does not pass or fail a rule unless the approved policy explicitly defines that treatment.

## `DATA_GAP`

Use when the analysis cannot be completed because identity, mapping, definitions, history, coverage, price, currency, or approval evidence is missing.

## `CONFLICT`

Use when authoritative sources disagree and no approved precedence rule resolves them. Quarantine the affected decision.

## Language guard

Say “observed usage,” “policy score,” and “candidate for review.” Do not upgrade these labels to buying intent, authority, confidence, probability, expected revenue, or a guaranteed outcome.
