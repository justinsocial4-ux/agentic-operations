# Contactability Policy

The customer owns legal/compliance interpretation. Record policy ID/version, owner, jurisdiction and message scope, required consent/lawful-basis evidence, suppression precedence, do-not-contact and bounce handling, owner/territory scope, cooldown, frequency cap/window, qualification values, and approval path.

Evaluate contactability before matching. Any explicit blocker prevents a preview recommendation. Missing required evidence yields `ELIGIBILITY_UNKNOWN`.

Qualification and an apparently valid address do not establish permission. Do not perform MX/network validation or external enrichment without separate authorization and privacy review.
