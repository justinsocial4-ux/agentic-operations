# Evidence Language

- `VERIFIED_SOURCE_VALUE`: dated source fact such as current title or suppression state.
- `POLICY_MAPPED_CATEGORY`: exact result from a named mapping version.
- `RECOMMENDED_PREVIEW`: unique policy match pending approval and execution-time recheck.
- `UNKNOWN`: required evidence absent or conflicting.

Say “routing persona,” not buyer/decision authority. Say “explicit demo request” or the exact approved event—not intent probability. Email opens and clicks need customer-approved reliability rules; missing activity is unknown, not Cold. Never manufacture confidence percentages.
