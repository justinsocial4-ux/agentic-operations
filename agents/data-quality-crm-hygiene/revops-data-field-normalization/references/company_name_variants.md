# Company Name Variants

Read when normalizing company legal suffixes and formatting.

Normalize case, spacing, punctuation, and approved suffixes such as `Inc`, `LLC`, `Ltd`, `Corp`, `GmbH`, and `S.A.` for comparison. Preserve the original display value and legal entity fields.

Do not collapse parent companies, subsidiaries, brands, franchises, or similarly named companies solely because their normalized strings match. Require account context and route ambiguity to review.
