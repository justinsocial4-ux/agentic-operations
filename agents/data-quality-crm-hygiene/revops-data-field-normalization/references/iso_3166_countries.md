# ISO 3166 Country Examples

Read when normalizing country names or codes. Validate against the current ISO source before production use.

| Common input | Alpha-2 | Alpha-3 | Canonical English name |
|---|---|---|---|
| US, USA, United States of America | US | USA | United States |
| UK, Great Britain | GB | GBR | United Kingdom |
| Canada | CA | CAN | Canada |
| Germany, Deutschland | DE | DEU | Germany |
| France | FR | FRA | France |

Do not infer a country from language, phone code, or company domain alone.
