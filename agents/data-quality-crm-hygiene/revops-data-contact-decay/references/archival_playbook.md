# Contact Archival Playbook

Use this reference before any archival plan or execution. It is an operational safety checklist, not legal advice.

## Non-negotiable rule

`ARCHIVED_CANDIDATE` means review recommended. It never means automatic archival. Obtain explicit user approval for the exact records and action.

## Pre-archive review

Confirm:

- the contact scores 86–100 under the approved model;
- the underlying evidence and timestamps are visible;
- engagement from every available channel was considered;
- duplicate activity was consolidated first;
- active deals, customers, VIPs, legal holds, and required suppression records are excluded;
- enrichment or human review did not find a current role or valid engagement path;
- the proposed action is reversible where the CRM permits it.

## Approval packet

Show the approver:

- record count and identifiers;
- decay score and primary drivers;
- data gaps and confidence limitations;
- proposed soft archive, list removal, or deletion behavior;
- downstream effects on campaigns, reporting, ownership, and integrations;
- recovery or restore procedure.

## Execution and receipt

After approval, act only on the approved list. Record approver, timestamp, run ID, before state, action result, failures, and recovery information. Stop and report partial failure rather than silently continuing.

## Compliance review

Retention, deletion, suppression, GDPR, CCPA, and industry obligations depend on the organization and jurisdiction. Confirm the customer's policy or qualified legal guidance. A marketing-inactive contact may still need to remain on a suppression or compliance record.
