# Privacy and identity

- Reject direct person, worker, customer, or company identity; contact details; domains; URLs; addresses; titles; activity; intent; protected data; special-category data; free text; candidate values; provider responses; and raw errors.
- Use stable pseudonymous record, field, provider, contract, source, route, and receipt IDs only.
- Require lawful-basis, notice, objection, suppression, correction, purpose, minimization, recipient, retention, and pseudonymization receipts.
- Do not claim GDPR, PECR, CCPA, security, privacy, accuracy, or regulatory compliance.
