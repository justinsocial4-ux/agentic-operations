# Population and lineage

Every metric contract declares the complete pseudonymous record-ID population. Submitted records must match it exactly once and bind to that contract's source, schema, authorization, query, page, population, period, freshness, definition, and approval receipts.

Reject duplicates, extras, omissions, stale or future evidence, mixed schemas, partial pages, cached substitutions, silent deduplication, direct identifiers, and free text. Preserve unresolved evidence states without numeric substitution.
