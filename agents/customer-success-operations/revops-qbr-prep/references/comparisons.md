# Comparisons

Compare only non-overlapping approved periods whose contracts match on metric definition, unit, currency, amount basis, aggregation rule, and timezone. Require an exact comparison-basis receipt and approval receipt. The helper calculates only current minus prior and the percent change.

Do not compare different cohorts, moving populations, created dates against event dates, product events against CRM activities, or commercial amounts with different bases. A zero prior value produces a null percent change.
