# Metric and time contracts

Treat every metric label as a customer-defined construct. Require exact unit, currency where applicable, amount basis, definition, aggregation rule, section slot, source/schema/query/page/population receipts, authorization window, approved local period and IANA timezone, freshness, and metric-owner approval.

Do not equate CRM activity with engagement, usage with value, support volume with sentiment, contract data with renewal intent, CRM amount with revenue, or a supplied score with validated health. Never infer missing dates, terms, targets, or values.
