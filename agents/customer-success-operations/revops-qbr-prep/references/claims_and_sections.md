# Claims and sections

The approved QBR blueprint lists exact section IDs and allowed metric, comparison, and claim slots. A claim entry carries only a customer-authored claim ID, template ID, evidence IDs, approval receipt, and helper-derived evidence state. The helper does not accept, create, rewrite, or display claim prose.

Section blocks are evidence-assembly receipts, not customer-facing slides or narrative. Do not invent titles, charts, speaker notes, causes, sentiment, forecasts, renewal strategy, expansion opportunities, conversation topics, or action items.
