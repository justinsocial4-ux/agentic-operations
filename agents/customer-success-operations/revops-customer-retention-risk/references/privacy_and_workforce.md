# Privacy And Workforce

Use only the minimum pseudonymous account evidence approved for this review. Reject names, domains, emails, phones, addresses, contact IDs, free text, ticket or survey text, message content, protected traits, accommodations, home location, and worker performance, quota, compensation, ranking, or coaching fields.

Account-level aggregation can still expose people or sensitive commercial behavior. Customer privacy, legal, security, data-governance, accessibility, and system owners determine lawful purpose, access, retention, deletion, correction, and downstream use. The skill does not declare GDPR, CCPA, HIPAA, SOX, ASC 606, contract, or employment compliance.

Do not use the review to judge, rank, coach, pay, promote, discipline, route work to, or monitor a CSM, seller, support worker, or customer contact.
