# Action Boundaries

The review is evidence, not authority. It never predicts churn or renewal; scores health or risk; ranks accounts; estimates ARR at risk, value, probability, confidence, forecast, urgency, severity, or intervention effect; diagnoses cause; selects a playbook; recommends outreach; grants a concession; or determines legal, finance, customer, or worker treatment.

Do not create or modify CRM fields, tasks, owners, messages, alerts, webhooks, schedules, contracts, bills, forecasts, queues, dashboards, or models. Do not contact, escalate, assign, coach, or monitor a person.

Named customer reviewers inspect unresolved receipts, correct source evidence through the approved path, and separately decide any authorized action outside this skill.

The final boundary is exactly: `NO CHURN OR RENEWAL PREDICTION / NO RANKING, CRM, OUTREACH, PLAYBOOK, FORECAST, OR WORKFORCE ACTION`.
