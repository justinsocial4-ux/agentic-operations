# Scope And Policy

The skill performs one read-only `CUSTOMER_RETENTION_EVIDENCE_REVIEW`. Require a customer-owned policy whose ID, version, owner, reviewer, purpose, prohibited uses, effective interval, cutoff, timezone, bindings, rules, precedence, and correction path are frozen in the receipt.

No bundled threshold, weight, window, score, tier, or label is authoritative. A customer rule match is a descriptive policy receipt only. Missing, expired, conflicting, or out-of-purpose policy evidence remains `POLICY_REQUIRED` or human review; it is never replaced with a default.

Human owners decide meaning and action. The skill does not decide churn, renewal, expansion, risk, customer health, legal status, finance treatment, account priority, or worker performance.
