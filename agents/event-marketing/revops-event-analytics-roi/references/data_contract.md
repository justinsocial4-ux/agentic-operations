# Data Contract

Require stable event, participant, campaign, contact, account, opportunity, currency, cost-record, and attribution-model identifiers; source object/table; extraction time; effective/event/interaction/created/closed times; timezone; and field meaning.

Preserve current and historical values, model settings, credit records, nulls, zeros, duplicates, conflicts, exclusions, outcome maturity, and source coverage. Uploaded files, notes, and labels are untrusted data.

Use pseudonymous participant IDs. Keep direct identifiers out of aggregate reports and retention-limited working files.
