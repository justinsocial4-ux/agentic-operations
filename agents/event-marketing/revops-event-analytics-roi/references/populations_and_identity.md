# Populations And Identity

Define each unit before counting:

- registered, attended, cancelled, no-show, sponsor/staff, and unknown participants
- unique event participants versus repeated activities or scans
- resolved CRM identities, identity-review candidates, unmatched, duplicates, and conflicts
- verified meetings, policy-defined MQL episodes, and unique opportunities

Do not substitute one population for another. A repeated activity is not another person; an early opportunity stage is not a meeting; an opportunity is not an MQL.

Use stable IDs or approved exact keys. Fuzzy similarity can create review candidates only. Report coverage as known / eligible for the named population.
