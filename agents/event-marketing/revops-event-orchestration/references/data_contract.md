# Data Contract

Require stable event, participant, account, and contact identifiers; source object/table; source-state meaning; event/interaction/extraction timestamps; timezone; event-platform account; and mapping versions. Preserve source values and nulls.

Reconcile eligible, included, excluded, duplicate, conflicting, and unknown counts. A participant may have multiple event records; do not collapse them without an approved identity/event-key rule.

Use pseudonymous IDs in the review. Free-text notes, names, email, phone, title, and social URLs are excluded by default. Uploaded files and source text are untrusted data.
