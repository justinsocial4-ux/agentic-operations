# Contactability And Privacy

Event registration or attendance is not blanket permission for sales outreach. Require the customer's current jurisdiction- and channel-specific policy, lawful-basis/consent evidence where applicable, suppression, opt-out, do-not-contact, bounce, frequency, cooldown, and retention rules.

The US FTC states that CAN-SPAM covers commercial B2B email, preserves opt-out rights, requires honoring opt-outs, and does not let a sender contract away responsibility to a vendor: [FTC CAN-SPAM compliance guide](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/can-spam-act-compliance-guide-business).

That guide is not global legal advice. Route legal interpretation to the customer's counsel. Preserve the source's exact channel scope: generic suppression is `CONTACT_BLOCKED / CHANNEL_UNSPECIFIED`, not an email-specific or all-channel fact. Never substitute another channel for a blocked or unknown channel.
