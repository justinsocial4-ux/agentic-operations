# Research And Claims

The legacy research failed four of seven independent QA checks. It contained unsupported booking/close-rate assumptions, unsourced data-quality thresholds, no required assumption-feasibility table, and self-check answers that contradicted the evidence.

Do not repeat its 60-times response, conversion, meeting, close-rate, labor, pipeline, ROI, match-rate, completeness, hot/warm/cold, 1-hour/24-hour, 5-to-7-day, 30-day, confidence, or calibration claims as facts or defaults.

Use customer records only for clearly defined descriptive measures. Observational differences across events, reps, segments, or response times do not establish causality. Prospective experiments require predeclared eligibility, assignment, outcomes, exclusions, analysis, and decision rules.
