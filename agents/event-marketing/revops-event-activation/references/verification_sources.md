# Verification sources

- ICO direct-marketing guidance: https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/direct-marketing-and-privacy-and-electronic-communications/direct-marketing-guidance/
- ICO collection, lead generation, and profiling: https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/direct-marketing-and-privacy-and-electronic-communications/direct-marketing-guidance/collect-information-and-generate-leads/
- Salesforce record-query permissions: https://developer.salesforce.com/docs/platform/graphql/guide/query-record-objects.html
- HubSpot workflow enrollment triggers: https://knowledge.hubspot.com/workflows/set-your-workflow-enrollment-triggers
- HubSpot marketing-event workflow limits: https://knowledge.hubspot.com/workflows/add-and-update-contacts-in-marketing-events-with-workflows
- NIST AI RMF trustworthiness characteristics: https://airc.nist.gov/airmf-resources/airmf/3-sec-characteristics/
- NIST AI RMF Core: https://airc.nist.gov/airmf-resources/airmf/5-sec-core/

These sources support contextual permissions, collection transparency, profiling limits, objection/suppression, account-specific workflow behavior, intended-use validation, safe failure, and human oversight. They do not validate universal badge consent, identity, enrichment accuracy, ICP fit, rep interest, buying stage, priority weights, tiers, conversion lift, outreach timing, contact action, CRM write, or workflow enrollment.
