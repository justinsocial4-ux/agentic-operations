# Privacy, identity, and profiling

- Reject direct person, worker, customer, or company identity; contact details; domains; URLs; addresses; titles; badge content; free text; rep notes; activity; behavior; interest; buying stage; firmographics; protected data; candidate values; enrichment responses; scores; tiers; conversions; and raw errors.
- Use stable pseudonymous participant, event, source, plan, route, role, channel, policy, and receipt IDs only.
- Require lawful-basis, collection/direct-marketing notice, objection, suppression, correction/access, purpose, minimization, recipient, retention, and pseudonymization evidence.
- Do not claim GDPR, PECR, CCPA, CAN-SPAM, security, privacy, consent, accuracy, fairness, or regulatory compliance.
