# Construct and interpretation

- A badge scan, import, form submission, attendance token, conversation, rep observation, title, company, timing, or later conversion is contextual evidence, not proof of identity, consent, interest, intent, fit, priority, readiness, contactability, or causality.
- Missing evidence is not a neutral score and cannot be converted into a tier, confidence discount, generic profile, or partial recommendation.
- Agreement, recency, vendor reputation, enrichment confidence, fuzzy similarity, and historical conversion do not establish truth for a person or authorize contact.
- Validate any future scoring or profiling construct independently on representative intended-use data before deployment; this skill contains no such construct.
