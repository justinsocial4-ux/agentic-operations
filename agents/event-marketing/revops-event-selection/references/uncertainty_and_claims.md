# Uncertainty And Claims

Report exact evidence states and receipts. Do not invent confidence percentages or translate counts into high/medium/low, good/poor, strong/weak, or sufficient/insufficient labels.

Do not infer event quality, account fit truth, buyer intent, competitor threat, demand, causality, pipeline, revenue, conversion, ROI, or future performance. Historical association is not causality; a criterion score is not model validation.

A predictive interpretation requires a separate model card, construct definition, training cutoff, leakage review, holdout/backtest, calibration, uncertainty method, and approved intended use. This helper intentionally provides none.
