# Scope And Policy

The helper performs one `EVENT_CRITERIA_COMPARISON` for one declared candidate population. Require stable policy ID/version, owner, reviewer, effective date, UTC cutoff, purpose, capacity, correction path, records system, and exact proposed human review.

Identity, criteria, cost, privacy, and downstream-use policy states must each be `APPROVED`. The final output never authorizes execution, even when a separate action status says approved.

Prohibited uses include automatic sponsorship approval, budget allocation, sales targeting, attendee treatment, employment decisions, and autonomous downstream orchestration.
