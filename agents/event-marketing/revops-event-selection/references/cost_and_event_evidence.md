# Cost And Event Evidence

Require event begin/end dates, one approved sponsorship-cost status, exact non-negative Decimal amount, currency, basis, and receipt. The policy defines accepted cost statuses and one currency. Do not mix currencies or convert without a separate approved dated FX receipt.

Dates and costs are descriptive evidence. Attendee estimates, historical sponsorships, public sponsor lists, and competitor presence do not prove attendance, intent, demand, pipeline, revenue, or return.

This helper does not calculate ROI, cost per lead, cost per opportunity, pipeline, closed-won value, or budget efficiency.
