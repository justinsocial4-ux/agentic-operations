# Action boundaries

The output is evidence for an authorized human review. It cannot prove compliance/readiness, infer enrollment, set or waive requirements, evaluate a worker, recommend outreach or escalation, or support a performance or employment decision.

Do not message, remind, alert, route, schedule, assign, escalate, write a system, notify a manager/executive, monitor response, or measure outcomes. `compliance_authorized`, `worker_judgment_authorized`, and `action_authorized` are always false.
