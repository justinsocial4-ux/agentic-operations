# Workforce and privacy

Require separate workforce, privacy, worker-notice, worker-access, worker-response, correction, appeal, recipient-policy, and retention-policy receipts. A vendor contract, LMS setting, CRM audit trail, manager approval, or generic legitimate-interest statement does not replace them.

Workers must have the customer's approved notice, access, response, correction, and appeal paths. The helper proves only receipt presence and binding; it does not claim legal compliance, fairness, readiness, or permission for an employment decision.
