# Delivery Policy

Analysis produces a preview only. Record exact deal, rep, playbook ID/version, safe link metadata, channel, timing, quiet hours, frequency/cooldown, rep preference, payload, named approver, and expiration.

Before separately authorized delivery, recheck deal context, content approval/version/active/access state, rep identity/preferences, quiet hours, and frequency. Any change voids the preview. Never post CRM cards/notes, Slack messages, email, SMS, tasks, or feedback records without exact authorization.
