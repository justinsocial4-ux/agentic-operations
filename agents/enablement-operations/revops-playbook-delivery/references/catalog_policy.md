# Catalog Policy

Record catalog ID/version, owner, approval workflow, active-date clock, supersession and withdrawal rules, content version, workspace, intended audiences, access groups, sensitivity, allowed locations, stable link, criteria, priority, and refresh receipt.

Exclude any playbook whose current approval, active status, version, location, or intended-rep access cannot be proven. Do not serve stale cache after a source/access failure. Do not retag, move, delete, or broaden access during analysis.
