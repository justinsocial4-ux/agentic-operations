# Workforce and privacy

Require separate workforce, privacy, worker-notice, worker-access, worker-response, correction, appeal, recipient-policy, and retention-policy receipts. A vendor contract or CRM audit trail does not replace them.

Workers must be informed through the customer's approved process and have approved access, response, correction, and appeal paths. The helper proves only receipt presence and binding; it does not claim legal compliance or that a process is fair.
