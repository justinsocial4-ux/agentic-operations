# Onboarding Policy

Require policy ID/version, owner, effective date, role/cohort, start-date anchor, milestone IDs, evidence source, expected days from start, day-zero convention, calendar/business clock, timezone, completion rule, and approved pause days.

HR owns leave, accommodation, transfer, termination, and protected-status treatment. Accept only an approved numeric pause/exclusion input; do not request or expose the reason.

Milestone states are descriptive record states. A late or missing record does not prove lack of skill, effort, readiness, or performance.
