# Evidence and Labels

- `VERIFIED_RECORD`: direct dated record from an approved source.
- `POLICY_DERIVATION`: exact date/count derived from verified inputs and named policy.
- `UNKNOWN_EVIDENCE`: source availability or coverage is insufficient.
- `CONFLICT`: approved sources disagree without a precedence resolution.

Allowed milestone states: `COMPLETED_ON_TIME`, `COMPLETED_LATE`, `DUE_NOT_RECORDED`, `PENDING`, `UNKNOWN_EVIDENCE`.

Do not use ready/unready, on-track/behind, risk, performance, effort, attrition, coaching, PIP, promotion, termination, or lead-routing labels. Activity logged in CRM is system evidence—not proof of work performed or buyer engagement.
