# Action and Access

The review is for authorized human managers/enablement/HR partners under customer policy. Minimize employee-level views and suppress small cohorts according to policy.

Prepare only neutral questions and previews. Include pseudonymous employee/cohort, evidence gap, policy/milestone version, reviewer, approver, retention, and exact proposed action. Never send alerts, create reminders/tasks, change assignments, recommend PIP/termination/promotion, or route leads based on this review.

Recheck access, employee scope, source corrections, policy version, and milestone evidence before any separately authorized action.
