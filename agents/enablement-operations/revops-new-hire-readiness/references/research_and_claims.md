# Research and Claims

Checked 2026-07-10. Legal and employment requirements vary; customer HR/legal policy controls.

- [EEOC: The ADA and software, algorithms, and AI used to assess job applicants and employees](https://www.eeoc.gov/laws/guidance/americans-disabilities-act-and-use-software-algorithms-and-artificial-intelligence) provides official US employment-assessment risk context.
- [NIST AI Risk Management Framework](https://www.nist.gov/itl/ai-risk-management-framework) provides risk-management and governance context.
- [ICO: Monitoring workers](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/employment/monitoring-workers/) provides UK data-protection context for worker monitoring.

These sources do not validate universal ramp curves, milestone days, risk labels, quota thresholds, activity rules, confidence scores, causal training claims, or employment decisions. The legacy research failed structural QA and is not evidence for those defaults.
