# Cohort Analysis

Predeclare population, role, policy version, hire window, observation cutoff, outcome maturity, territory/product, quota basis/currency, leave/transfer treatment, exclusions, confounders, metrics, uncertainty method, and decision limits.

Keep unresolved and immature outcomes visible. Report counts, denominators, missingness, means/medians, differences, and uncertainty only when valid. Observational differences between training completers and non-completers may reflect selection, manager, territory, prior experience, tenure, quota, or data quality.

Do not call association causal, statistically meaningful without a preapproved method, or sufficient for mandatory training or employment action.
