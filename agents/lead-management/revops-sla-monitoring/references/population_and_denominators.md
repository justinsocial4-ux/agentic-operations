# Population and denominators

Freeze the full in-scope record population at the cutoff. Preserve exclusions with reason, evidence, and policy IDs. Keep duplicate, unknown, pending, policy-required, source-required, conflicting, and unassociated lanes visible.

The resolved denominator contains only `MET` plus `BREACHED` records. `PENDING` is not compliant and is not breached. Excluded and unresolved records do not enter the rate denominator, but their counts remain in the report.

Never treat missing events as zero response time, late events as on time, or a partial extraction as the full population.
