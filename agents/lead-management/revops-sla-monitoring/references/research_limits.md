# Research limits

Do not repeat inherited claims about universal response windows, qualification lift, revenue loss, time saved, forecast improvement, rep behavior, tool pricing, API limits, setup time, or production readiness. The research brief's vendor material, arithmetic chains, and self-check do not validate those claims for a customer.

Do not convert citation presence into model validity. Validate the customer's policy construct, population, event semantics, calendar, exclusions, and denominator before reporting an SLA state.

Record verified source, version/date, and remaining uncertainty. This skill does not provide legal advice, certify compliance, or establish an employment-performance measure.
