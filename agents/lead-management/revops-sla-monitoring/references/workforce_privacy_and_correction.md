# Workforce, privacy, and correction

SLA evidence can affect workload, coaching, discipline, pay, promotion, and performance evaluation. Use pseudonymous owner IDs and approved work evidence only. Exclude names, emails, message/call content, home locations, protected traits, manager narratives, quota, discipline, and historical performance by default.

Require authorized purpose/viewers, retention, correction/appeal path, affected-party review, accessibility and labor review where applicable, and named human approval before any consequential use.

NIST AI RMF calls for explicit human roles, oversight, construct validation, impact mapping, and feedback: https://airc.nist.gov/airmf-resources/airmf/5-sec-core/ . EEOC notes that software used to monitor performance or inform pay/promotion can create employment-discrimination risk: https://www.eeoc.gov/newsroom/us-eeoc-and-us-department-justice-warn-against-disability-discrimination . These sources support governance, not a compliance verdict.
