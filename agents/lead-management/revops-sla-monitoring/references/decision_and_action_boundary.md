# Decision and action boundary

The output is evidence for human review. Do not create CRM fields, update SLA status, reassign ownership, create tasks, send Slack/email, schedule jobs, trigger workflows, notify a manager, or escalate a record.

Do not rank reps or teams, label performance, recommend coaching/discipline, or infer intent, productivity, revenue impact, forecast impact, morale, retention, or quota effects.

Any later alert or workflow requires a separate approved implementation plan with source permissions, idempotency, recipient policy, quiet hours, correction/appeal, rollback, monitoring, and named authorization. End here with `NO ALERT / NO ESCALATION / NO CRM OR WORKFORCE ACTION`.
