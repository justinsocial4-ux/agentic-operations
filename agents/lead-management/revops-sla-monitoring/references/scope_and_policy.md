# Scope and policy

Freeze the review ID, purpose, SLA policy ID/version, start event, qualifying stop-event types, clock type, target, timezone, cutoff, exclusions, denominator, owner, reviewer, approver, correction path, retention, and prohibited uses.

An SLA is customer policy, not an industry constant. Do not import source-specific minutes, stage days, warning bands, grace periods, or escalation multipliers from the legacy skill or market material. If policy is absent or ambiguous, return `POLICY_REQUIRED`.

This skill validates supplied evidence only. It does not activate a policy, change a workflow, or decide what the business should promise.
