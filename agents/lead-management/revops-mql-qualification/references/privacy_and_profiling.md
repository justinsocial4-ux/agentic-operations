# Privacy and profiling

Use pseudonymous record, condition, source, population, role, and receipt IDs. Reject direct identity, contact details, URLs, domains, free text, message content, protected traits, special-category data, worker data, and seller-performance data.

Behavior-based direct-marketing qualification is profiling. Require approved purpose, lawful basis, notice, accuracy/correction, objection, recipient, retention, and human-review controls before accepting a condition set. Never infer behavior or identity from missing evidence.
