# Privacy and marketing

Behavioral profiling for marketing requires a documented lawful basis, fair and transparent collection, accurate and minimized data, notice, preference/objection handling, and due diligence on third-party sources. A vendor promise or CRM consent flag is not a complete receipt.

This skill therefore accepts pseudonymous organization observations only. It does not identify people, append contact details, infer interests, decide contactability, or execute direct marketing. Suppression and unauthorized lanes remain visible without exposing the underlying person or behavior.
