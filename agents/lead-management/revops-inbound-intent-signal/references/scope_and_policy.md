# Scope and policy

The approved purpose is `pseudonymous_inbound_signal_evidence_review`. The policy must be effective at the cutoff and must freeze a UTC observation window, timezone, source bindings, event rules, privacy/marketing approvals, notice program, human reviewer, correction path, objection path, and prohibited uses. No default policy or inferred authority is allowed.

The review answers only: “Does each supplied pseudonymous observation have the exact evidence and lineage required by the approved descriptive rule?” It does not answer whether anyone intends to buy.
