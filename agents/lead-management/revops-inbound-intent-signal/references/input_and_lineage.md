# Input and lineage

Use stable lowercase IDs. Organization IDs must begin `anonymous-org-`. Do not accept direct or reversible identifiers, domains, URLs, IP addresses, cookies, device IDs, CRM IDs, names, contact data, titles, or free text.

Every source binding requires exact source/version, schema/version, authorization window, allowed event types, and receipts for collection authority, purpose compatibility, notice, objection synchronization, and retention. Every complete population declares its observation IDs. Every observation repeats the lineage exactly and supplies one allowed evidence lane.
