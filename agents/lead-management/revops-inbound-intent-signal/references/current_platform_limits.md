# Current platform limits

Do not assume a universal CRM or analytics schema. Salesforce metadata and access vary by org, API version, object, field, record type, and context-user permissions. Validate exported evidence before this skill; the skill itself has no connector.

Google Analytics 4 uses IP addresses during collection for coarse location and discards them before logging. Do not claim a default GA4 export can provide IP addresses for company or person resolution. Vendor intent and review-site exports also require their own exact contracts and authorizations.
