# Action and Privacy Limits

Read before discussing remediation, alerts, savings, or workforce use.

- No API/IAM/CRM writes, provisioning, deactivation, permission removal, credential rotation, tickets, tasks, alerts, messages, schedules, PDFs, attestations, or archives.
- No names, emails, phones, fuzzy identity matching, HR diagnoses, employment conclusions, productivity labels, manager outreach, coaching, or workforce decisions.
- No cost recovery, license reclaimability, breach impact, ranking, severity, confidence, compliance verdict, due date, owner assignment, or recommended action.
- Do not push access outputs into customer-risk, account-scoring, or employee-monitoring systems.
- End at: `NO COMPLIANCE VERDICT / NO RISK OR SAVINGS SCORE / NO ACCESS, TICKET, MESSAGE, SCHEDULE, OR WORKFORCE ACTION`.
