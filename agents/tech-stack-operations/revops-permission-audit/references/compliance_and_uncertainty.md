# Compliance and Uncertainty

Read before discussing effective access, risk, confidence, severity, or compliance.

- `OBSERVED_ENTITLEMENT_COMPARISON` means only the supplied entitlement IDs were compared. It is not proof of effective access across all platform mechanisms.
- Do not manufacture confidence, probability, severity, risk, or priority from counts, labels, activity age, or role names.
- An access-review packet can support an authorized review but cannot certify SOC 2, HIPAA, GDPR, PCI, SOX, or another framework.
- Applicability, criteria, control design, operating effectiveness, evidence sufficiency, retention, and auditor acceptance remain customer/auditor/legal decisions.
- R1 prohibits unapproved adequacy adjectives. Report exact states, counts, receipts, and unresolved lanes.
