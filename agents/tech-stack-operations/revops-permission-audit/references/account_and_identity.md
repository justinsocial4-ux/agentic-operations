# Account and Identity

Read before joining identities or classifying accounts.

- Use stable pseudonymous `account_id` and `subject_id` receipts. Do not accept names, emails, phone numbers, job-performance data, or fuzzy identity matches.
- Preserve customer-defined account types such as individual, shared, group, service, integration, emergency, guest, developer, temporary, and contractor.
- Do not infer account type, employment status, ownership, purpose, or authorization from a label, domain, last activity, or platform state.
- Shared/service/emergency accounts require their own organization-defined conditions, accountable owner role, and review policy.
- A missing or conflicting identity link stays unresolved and does not erase valid entitlement evidence.
