# Scope and Policy

Read before accepting the requested decision or control context.

- The supported job is a read-only comparison of supplied evidence, not a live IAM audit or compliance examination.
- Freeze policy, source/schema/namespace, cutoff, population totals, account types, access and exception rules, authorization source, activity rule, control context, privacy/workforce scope, roles, correction path, and prohibited uses.
- Require named owner, reviewer, and approver roles. `APPROVAL_REQUIRED` never authorizes access change.
- Treat control mappings as customer-supplied context only. The packet is not a SOC report, certification, attestation, violation, legal opinion, or auditor acceptance decision.
- New evidence or corrections create a new review version.
