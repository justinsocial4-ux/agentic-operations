# Cost, Contract, And Decision Boundaries

Keep contracted fee, committed minimum, order form, invoice, tax, allocation, bundle, discount, consumption charge, paid cash, renewal quote, and forecast separate. Require currency, period, license basis, source, and dated FX policy if conversion is approved.

An unassigned or unobserved entitlement is not automatically removable. Verify tier floors, bundle dependencies, minimums, true-ups, notice dates, growth plans, contingent access, critical infrequent workflows, and contract terms.

Renewal, right-sizing, consolidation, replacement, training, security, and vendor-contact options require an approved decision rule and named approver. Consolidation additionally needs workflow dependency, feature/configuration, accessibility, integration, data/retention, security/privacy, continuity, switching, stakeholder, and migration evidence.

Without those inputs, return evidence-backed questions such as “Which assigned identities are unmatched?” or “Does the contract permit reducing paid seats?” Do not recommend a quantity, discount, tool, timeline, or action.

Never send a message, create a task, alter a license, contact a vendor, change a renewal, or write a system record.
