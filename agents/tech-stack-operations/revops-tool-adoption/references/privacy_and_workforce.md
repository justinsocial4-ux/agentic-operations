# Privacy And Workforce Boundaries

Default to aggregate tool-level evidence. Use person-level data only when an approved policy names the purpose, lawful/policy basis as reviewed by the customer's authorized expert, authorized viewer, minimum fields, suppression rules, retention, access logging, correction path, and prohibited downstream uses.

Use pseudonymous stable IDs when record-level reconciliation is necessary. Do not include names, emails, IP addresses, content, titles, messages, files, channels, locations, manager relationships, or free text by default.

Never use utilization evidence to infer employee performance, engagement, intent, attendance, misconduct, productivity, capability, compensation, discipline, termination, or promotion. Do not rank or label people.

Withheld or privacy-concealed identities remain `WITHHELD`; they are not inactive. Small groups remain suppressed according to customer policy.

When person-level reporting is disabled, do not ask the report reader to identify unmatched, unknown, or conflicting people. Assign aggregate reconciliation to the authorized identity steward and publish only refreshed counts and evidence provenance.

The NIST Privacy Framework supports enterprise privacy-risk management; it does not certify legal compliance. Route jurisdiction- and employment-specific conclusions to authorized privacy/legal/HR reviewers.
