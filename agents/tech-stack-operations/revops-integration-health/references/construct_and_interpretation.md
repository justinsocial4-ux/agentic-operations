# Construct and interpretation

- Endpoint reachability is not end-to-end integration correctness.
- A connector job state is not source or destination correctness.
- A recent checkpoint is not proof of a complete population or correct fields.
- Equal totals are not proof of record identity, semantic equivalence, or absence of duplicated/omitted records.
- Sequential failures do not establish root cause or cascade. Operational metadata does not establish forecast, reporting, revenue, customer, or workforce impact.
- Return exact contract evidence states only. Humans validate meaning and decide incident status.
