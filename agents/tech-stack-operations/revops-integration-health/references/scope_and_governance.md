# Scope and governance

- Require one effective customer policy, one declared pseudonymous integration population, one approved recipient, and named human operations/security/privacy reviewers.
- Keep purpose limited to reviewing supplied observations against frozen customer contracts.
- Require separate operations approval, security review, privacy review, recipient, retention, and observation-policy receipts.
- Corrected policy or evidence starts a new frozen review. This skill never declares an incident or authorizes action.
