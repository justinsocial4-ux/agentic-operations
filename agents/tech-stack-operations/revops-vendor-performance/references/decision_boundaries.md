# Decision And Action Boundaries

## Observation before option

First present requirement states, context lanes, conflicts, and gaps. Then, only when supplied, apply an approved decision rule with ID/version, owner, effective date, inputs, and named approver.

Without a rule, return `RULE_REQUIRED` and list review options as questions—not recommendations.

## Preview-only options

Possible preview labels include:

- request evidence clarification;
- validate incident scope;
- review a clause-backed credit candidate;
- schedule service review/QBR;
- begin a security/privacy review;
- compare renewal terms;
- evaluate alternatives and transition risk.

Every option must link to evidence IDs, state what it would and would not decide, name the owner/approver, and remain reversible until approved.

## Prohibited execution

Do not contact the vendor, submit a claim, send a breach notice, accept terms, change auto-renewal, update procurement/CRM, approve security, open an RFP, migrate data, cancel licenses, or alter spend.

Legal interpretation, breach notice, credit entitlement, termination rights, data-processing compliance, and regulatory conclusions require qualified review.

## Decision receipt

Record decision question, rule ID/version or `RULE_REQUIRED`, supporting and contrary evidence IDs, unknowns, options, reversibility, owner, approver, review date, and `NO EXTERNAL ACTION`.
