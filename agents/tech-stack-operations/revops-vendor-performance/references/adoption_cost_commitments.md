# Adoption, Cost, Credits, And Commitments

## Adoption

Define licensed, provisioned, eligible, active, human, admin, test, bot, contractor, and duplicate populations. State activity event, window, identity coverage, and denominator.

Active-seat rate is descriptive customer utilization. It does not prove vendor quality, product value, waste, causality, shelfware, or whether renewal is appropriate. Keep changes in seats, roles, seasonality, rollout, policy, and data coverage visible.

## Cost

Separate contracted fee, order form, invoice, tax, allocation, discount, usage charge, paid cash, committed minimum, forecast, and renewal quote. Every amount needs currency, period, basis, source, and dated FX policy if conversion is approved.

Do not invent market medians, typical increases, avoided cost, switching cost, or savings.

## Credits

Require clause ID/version, eligible service/period, breach evidence, claim window, formula, caps, exclusions, currency, and reviewer. A helper calculation may produce `CREDIT_CANDIDATE`; it is not legal entitlement, approval, invoice offset, or cash received.

## Product commitments

Use an approved commitment ledger containing ID, source, accepted scope, accountable party, promised date, change history, status evidence, and materiality policy. Public roadmap items, ideas, feature requests, and sales statements remain separate unless an approved policy makes them commitments.

Do not assign equal points to unlike features or penalize missing public roadmap data.
