# Comparison Basis

A customer-approved basis fixes product scope, configuration scope, quantity, unit, currency, price kind, billing basis, billing period, contract term, region, tax basis, fee basis, and discount basis under a stable ID/version.

Two observations are comparable only when both reference the same basis and are active at the declared comparison time. Do not infer package equivalence, currency conversion, annualization, unit normalization, tax allocation, or discount equivalence.
