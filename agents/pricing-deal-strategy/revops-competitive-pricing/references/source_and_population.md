# Source and Population

Each lane/source requires an exact authorization ID, schema version, and one complete population receipt. All population observation IDs must be unique, declared, and partition the frozen declared population exactly.

`public_list`, `supplied_quote`, `transcript_code`, `review_code`, and `internal_offer` are separate evidence lanes. Do not scrape or parse them here. Missing or incomplete source evidence cannot prove zero, absence, or a price.
