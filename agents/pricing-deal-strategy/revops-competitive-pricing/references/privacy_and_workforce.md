# Privacy and Workforce Boundary

Use stable pseudonymous party, competitor, item, source, observation, and role IDs. Reject names, domains, URLs, contact details, free text, notes, transcripts, quotes, reviews, posts, job titles, protected traits, and precise worker identity.

Do not compare, rank, coach, reassign, compensate, monitor, or evaluate sellers or managers. Do not expose customer negotiation content or confidential terms outside the approved structured receipts.
