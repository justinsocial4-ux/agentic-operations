# Pricing Math

Use plain Decimal strings. Store catalog list unit price, quoted unit price, quantity, and any supplied discount as separate facts. Derive extended list amount, quoted amount, discount amount, and discount percentage only when unit, currency, basis, billing period, versions, and positive list price match exactly.

Discount is not gross margin. Do not infer cost, tax, fee, shipping, FX, TCV, ARR, ACV, MRR, recognized revenue, payment, profitability, or willingness to pay. Do not combine currencies or one-time, recurring, ramp, usage, future, tax, fee, shipping, or indefinite-term components. Exact groups remain separate.
