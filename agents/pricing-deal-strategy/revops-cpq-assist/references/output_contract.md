# Output Contract

Return one deterministic JSON document containing the review type/state, policy receipt, declared population, catalog and quote-line receipts, numeric facts, exact amount groups, approval-process receipts, rule-result references, and fixed boundary.

Return helper stdout byte for byte, from `{` through the final newline after `}`. Add no prose, fence, table, interpretation, correction, or second rendering. Every numeric value appears once in its helper-owned fact or policy-rule receipt; other sections refer by ID.
