# Scope And Policy

Run one read-only `QUOTE_CONFIGURATION_EVIDENCE_REVIEW`. Require a customer-owned policy whose ID, version, owner, reviewer role, purpose, prohibited uses, effective interval, cutoff, timezone, catalog/pricebook/source bindings, rules, precedence, and correction path are frozen in the receipt.

No bundled product, price, discount, term, commercial tier, approver, SLA, or action is authoritative. A rule match is a policy observation, not compliance, approval, authorization, or permission to act. Missing, expired, overlapping, or conflicting policy evidence remains unresolved.
