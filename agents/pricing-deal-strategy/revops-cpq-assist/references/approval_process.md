# Approval Process

Approval evidence is descriptive. Preserve exact process ID/version, active state, applicable rule IDs, reviewer-role IDs, `ALL`, `ANY`, `SEQUENTIAL`, or `PARALLEL` mode, permission receipt, side-effect receipt, source/schema, timestamp, and observed state.

Do not resolve people, suppress obligations, calculate deadlines, create links, submit, approve, reject, request changes, route, notify, or escalate. A matched observation is not authorization or approval.
