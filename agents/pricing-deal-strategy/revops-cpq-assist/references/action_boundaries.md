# Action Boundaries

The receipt is evidence, not authority. It never recommends or ranks products/bundles, invents a SKU/price/term, optimizes discount, estimates margin/outcomes, declares compliance, approves/rejects/submits/routes/escalates, selects a person, creates a customer quote/PDF/link, sends a message, or changes CRM/CPQ/contract/invoice/subscription/workforce state.

The final boundary is exactly: `NO PRODUCT OR PRICE RECOMMENDATION / NO APPROVAL, CUSTOMER SEND, CRM, LEGAL, FINANCE, OR WORKFORCE ACTION`.
