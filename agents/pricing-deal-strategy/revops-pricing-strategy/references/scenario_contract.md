# Scenario contract

A `customer_scenario` observation is a customer-authored hypothetical amount with an approved scenario policy, source binding, basis, effective window, and population receipt. It is not a recommendation, forecast, offer, approved quote, or authorization to use the amount.

The helper compares one scenario with one approved internal observation only when their complete bases and effective windows match. Human finance, legal, pricing, and commercial owners decide whether a scenario should be tested or adopted.
