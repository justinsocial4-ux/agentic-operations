# Scope and governance

Require one effective policy for the exact internal pricing scenario evidence-review purpose. Bind the owner, human reviewer, finance approval, legal/competition approval, privacy approval, workforce approval, recipient policy, retention policy, scenario policy, cutoff, timezone, correction path, calculation policy, source bindings, and comparison bases.

System access, a job title, a prior pricing deck, or a sales request does not grant price-setting, approval, publication, or downstream-decision authority.
