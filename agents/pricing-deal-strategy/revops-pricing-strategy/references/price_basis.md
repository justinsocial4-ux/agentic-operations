# Price basis

Compare amounts only when one approved basis proves the same product, configuration, quantity, unit, currency, price kind, billing basis, billing period, contract term, region, tax basis, fee basis, and discount basis.

List, quote, final, contract, booked, billed, collected, and recognized-revenue amounts are not interchangeable. Never infer list price from a median, derive a discount without the exact approved operands, convert currency, annualize an unknown term, or normalize packages.
