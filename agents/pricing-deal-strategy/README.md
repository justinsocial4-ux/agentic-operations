# Pricing And Deal Strategy

These agents inspect pricing, quote configuration, and commercial-policy evidence without changing live systems or making approval decisions.

| Agent | What it does |
|---|---|
| [CPQ Assist Agent](revops-cpq-assist/SKILL.md) | Reviews exact quote, catalog, pricing-rule, and approval-process evidence without recommending products or taking action. |
| [Competitive Price Evidence Review](revops-competitive-pricing/SKILL.md) | Compares complete structured price observations only when their customer-approved product, quantity, currency, term, tax, fee, and discount basis matches exactly. |
| [Internal Pricing Scenario Evidence Review](revops-pricing-strategy/SKILL.md) | Compares approved internal price evidence with customer-authored scenarios on one exact basis without inferring an optimal price or authorizing action. |
