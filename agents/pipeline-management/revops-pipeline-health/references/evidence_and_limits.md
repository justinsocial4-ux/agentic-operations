# PM-01 Evidence And Limits

Read before citing benchmarks, promising improvement, or estimating forecast impact.

## Current Support

Salesforce's current Pipeline Inspection documentation supports using Amount, Close Date, Next Step, Owner, Recent Activity, Stage, Days In Stage, push counts, and change metrics to inspect pipeline. It also notes that activity-data availability depends on configuration and product changes.

Salesforce flags opportunities whose Next Step has not changed in seven or more days and exposes recent activity and stage-age information. Those product behaviors support the qualitative audit design, not PM-01's custom score or outcome claims.

## Unsupported Legacy Figures

Do not repeat the old exact forecast-variance, labor-cost, tool-price, zombie-pipeline, conversion, coverage, or revenue-impact claims without a current direct source and matching population. The project's own PM-01 QA audit already failed the Gartner ranking and $75K labor basis.

## Customer-Validated Defaults

- 40/30/30 score weights;
- 60/40 status thresholds;
- 80% minimum evidence coverage;
- stage stall thresholds;
- seven-day grace period;
- placeholder list;
- required optional fields;
- expected stage durations;
- alert cadence.
