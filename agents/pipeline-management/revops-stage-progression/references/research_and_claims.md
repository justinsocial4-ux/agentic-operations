# Research and Claims

Checked 2026-07-10.

- [Salesforce Opportunity History](https://help.salesforce.com/s/articleView?id=sales.opp_history.htm&language=en_US&type=5) documents history entries for Stage, Amount, Probability, and Close Date.
- [Salesforce stage-duration reporting](https://help.salesforce.com/s/articleView?id=000392663&language=en_US&type=1) notes that repeated stage visits can differ from accumulated path time.
- [HubSpot custom funnel reports](https://knowledge.hubspot.com/reports/create-custom-funnel-reports) documents all-stage versus any-stage inclusion logic.
- [HubSpot funnel builder](https://knowledge.hubspot.com/reports/create-new-custom-funnel-reports) uses automatically updated lifecycle/deal stage-entry timestamps.

These sources support history and funnel mechanics. They do not establish universal stage order, dwell thresholds, sample size, anomaly weights, confidence, sandbagging detection, causal coaching, forecast impact, labor savings, or accuracy improvement. Exclude the legacy defaults and promises.
