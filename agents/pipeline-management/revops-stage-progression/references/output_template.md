# Output Template

1. Preflight: policy, cutoff, pipeline, clock, stages, benchmark, source, amount basis.
2. Coverage: eligible deals, covered deals, events, missing history, unknown stages, conflicts.
3. Episodes: deal, stage, entry, exit/cutoff, days, benchmark, ratio, dwell label.
4. Transitions: deal, timestamp, from/to, classification, policy rule, evidence label.
5. Limitations: unknown causes, unavailable motive/risk/forecast conclusions, CRM amount basis.
6. Preview: neutral questions, owner, approver, `NO CRM WRITE` receipt.
