# Evidence and Language

- `RECORDED_EVENT`: dated CRM stage-entry evidence.
- `POLICY_EXCEPTION`: recorded event that violates a preapproved transition/timing rule.
- `HYPOTHESIS`: a possible explanation requiring separate evidence.
- `UNKNOWN`: absent, conflicting, or uninstrumented evidence.

Use `EXTENDED_DWELL`, `RAPID_TRANSITION`, `REGRESSION`, and `POLICY_SKIP`. Do not say zombie, suspicious, sandbagging, unqualified, buyer concern, at risk, confidence, or forecast impact from stage history alone.

Ask neutral questions and cite buyer/seller events before explaining cause. Report CRM amount only under its approved basis and currency.
