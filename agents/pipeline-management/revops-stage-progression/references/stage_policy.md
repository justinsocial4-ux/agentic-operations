# Stage Policy

Record a versioned policy containing stable stage IDs/order, labels, terminal states, reopen rules, allowed skip/regression/administrative transitions, pipeline-change treatment, timezone, clock, benchmark cohort/statistic/window, preapproved dwell thresholds, missing-data treatment, and approver.

A skip is only a policy exception when the intermediate stage is required. A backward move is a recorded regression in stage order, not proof of lost qualification. Re-entry stays a new episode. Thresholds created after reviewing results govern only future reviews.
