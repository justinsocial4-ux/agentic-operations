# Action Boundaries

The output authorizes no CRM query or update, forecast/category/probability change, stage/date/amount/owner edit, task, ticket, message, alert, schedule, call, email, coaching, escalation, compensation, discipline, or downstream workflow.

Human review may correct evidence or approve a separate controlled action. That later workflow requires current evidence, named authority, platform permissions, privacy/workforce review, and an auditable reversal path.

The final boundary is `NO FORECAST OR DEAL VERDICT / NO CRM, OWNER, COACHING, TASK, MESSAGE, ALERT, SCHEDULE, OR WORKFORCE ACTION`.
