# Privacy And Workforce

Use pseudonymous deal, account, owner, source, and evidence IDs. Reject account/deal/person names, emails, phones, titles, message/call content, sentiment, notes, transcripts, and free text.

Deal evidence can be used to monitor or evaluate workers when connected to owners, coaching, compensation, promotion, discipline, or termination. Require approved privacy and workforce policies, a named reviewer, correction path, and explicit prohibition on automated employment decisions.

Never rank owners, compare reps, infer effort, prescribe coaching, or emit manager-level performance judgments.
