# Source And Population

Require one frozen current-state snapshot and registered source ID/version, schema ID/version, extraction ID, UTC cutoff, population receipt, declared count, and per-deal membership receipt.

Current records, field history, opportunity stage history, historical-trending snapshots, HubSpot property history, activities, and associations have different semantics. The policy names which source supplies each lane; never merge them by analogy.

Future, stale, unregistered, duplicate, incomplete, or conflicting evidence makes the affected lane unresolved. A complete deal population does not imply complete stage or activity history.
