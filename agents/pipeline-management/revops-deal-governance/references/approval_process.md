# Approval-process evidence

Approval behavior is customer and platform configuration. Require process ID/version, observation key/state, source/schema, observed timestamp, permissions receipt, side-effects receipt, and exact applicable policy rule.

Entry criteria, allowed submitters, approver roles, sequence, parallelism, edition/licence, record locking, notifications, tasks, field updates, and flows must be supplied—not inferred. A matched process rule is evidence for human review, not authorization to submit or act.

Never look up an approver, assign a person, calculate a due time, submit for approval, approve, reject, escalate, notify, or write status.
