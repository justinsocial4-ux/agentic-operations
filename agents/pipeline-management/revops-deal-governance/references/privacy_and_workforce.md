# Privacy and workforce boundary

Use pseudonymous deal, account, owner, policy-owner, and reviewer-role IDs. Minimize contract, customer, seller, and approver data to what the approved review requires.

Reject direct identity, contact details, free text, message or call content, protected traits, disability or accommodation data, home location, rep seniority, historical performance, compensation, quota, manager ranking, and coaching fields. Do not infer seller effort, intent, quality, discipline, or training need.

Access approval, purpose limitation, retention, correction, and named human review remain customer-owned. The helper performs no workforce or legal determination.
