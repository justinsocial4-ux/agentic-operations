# Forecast Types

Read before naming any forecast number.

- **Closed-won amount:** CRM amount for units closed won within the period under the approved amount-basis policy. It is not recognized revenue, earned revenue, cash, or guaranteed collection unless a finance-controlled source says so.
- **Category rollup:** sum of snapshot amounts in an exclusive CRM category.
- **Submitted forecast:** seller or manager judgment recorded for a period.
- **Weighted expectation:** sum of amount times approved calibrated probability.
- **Scenario:** downside/base/upside result under explicit assumptions.
- **Goal gap:** approved goal minus the chosen, named forecast measure.

These measures can differ legitimately. Show them side by side; do not reconcile by silently renaming one. Name the amount basis explicitly, such as bookings, ARR, ACV, TCV, units, or another approved measure.

Probabilities must match the target: probability of closing won in the forecast period as of the cutoff. Stage-to-next-stage rates, lifetime win rates, seller category labels, and CRM default probabilities are not interchangeable without validation.
