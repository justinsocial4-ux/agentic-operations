# Output Template

Read before producing the report.

1. **Preflight receipt:** decision, source, cutoff, period, policy, scope, amount, currency/FX, categories, probability/model, completeness, exclusions.
2. **Category/closed-won table:** category ID/label, count, amount, and closed-won amount under the named CRM basis. Do not call it realized, booked, recognized, earned, billed, collected, cash, or guaranteed without finance evidence.
3. **Forecast measures:** seller submission, manager submission, weighted expectation, coverage, provenance—each separately named.
4. **Scenarios/goals/deltas:** approved assumptions, ordering, comparable snapshots, reconciled/unknown drivers.
5. **Backtest/bias:** period count, MAE, WAPE, signed bias, zero-denominator states, policy changes.
6. **Action and validation:** evidence, unknowns, human steps, exact reconciliation, no-write statement.

Never display a confidence percentage unless it is itself the output of an approved, validated model with documented calibration. Evidence coverage is not confidence.
