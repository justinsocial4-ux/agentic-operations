# Research and Claim Status

Read before repeating platform, benchmark, lift, labor, or accuracy claims.

Primary documentation:

- Salesforce Forecasting supports configurable forecast types and projections: <https://help.salesforce.com/s/articleView?id=newforecasts_overview.htm&language=en_US&type=5>
- Salesforce recommends aligning forecast categories/probabilities to opportunity stages: <https://help.salesforce.com/s/articleView?id=000392673&language=en_US&type=1>
- Salesforce supports custom dates/measures, groups, splits, products, and territories: <https://help.salesforce.com/s/articleView?id=sf.forecasts3_forecast_types_overview.htm&language=en_US>
- HubSpot's forecast tool uses deal stages or configured forecast categories and applies account exchange-rate rules: <https://knowledge.hubspot.com/forecast/use-the-forecast-tool>
- HubSpot categories are configurable, mapped per pipeline, and manually editable: <https://knowledge.hubspot.com/forecast/set-up-the-forecast-tool>

These sources support configurable forecast mechanics. They do not support the legacy ±30% to ±5–10% promise, arbitrary scenario weights, universal sample/data thresholds, fake confidence score, rep-coaching labels, or accuracy-improvement claims. Exclude those from decisions.
