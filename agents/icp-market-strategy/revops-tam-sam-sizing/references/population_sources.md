# Population sources

Record dataset/source/version/year, as-of and extraction time, unit, coverage, geography, taxonomy/version/code, count state, and evidence ID for every segment.

Firms, establishments, legal accounts, locations, contacts, employees, and vendor records differ. U.S. Census CBP reports establishments with paid employees by geography and NAICS; SUSB separately reports firms and establishments by establishment industry and enterprise size. Neither is automatically a prospect count.

Preserve suppression, exclusion, nonsampling/coverage limits, nonemployer treatment, multi-establishment treatment, source age, and taxonomy changes. Never convert unavailable or suppressed values to zero.

Official context: https://www.census.gov/data/developers/data-sets/cbp-zbp/cbp-api.html and https://www.census.gov/programs-surveys/susb.html .
