# Scope and policy

Freeze the purpose, review/policy IDs and versions, cutoff, total/serviceable/obtainable definitions, target count unit, coverage definition, taxonomy/geography schemes, price currency/period/basis, freshness rule, overlap/aggregation rule, obtainability horizon, roles, correction path, retention/access scope, and prohibited uses.

TAM, SAM, and SOM are not self-defining universal measures. Treat each as a customer-owned scenario definition. Return `POLICY_REQUIRED` when a definition or owner is absent.

The review validates supplied evidence only. It does not decide which market to pursue or certify that the source universe is complete.
