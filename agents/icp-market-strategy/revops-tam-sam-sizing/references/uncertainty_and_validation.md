# Uncertainty and validation

Report source states, coverage, suppression, age, mismatches, and unresolved lanes. Do not manufacture a confidence percentage or adequacy label from record counts, completeness, freshness, convergence, or model preference.

Validation must be tied to the same unit, population, definitions, and deployment decision. A top-down number agreeing with a bottom-up number does not prove either is correct when they share assumptions or coverage error.

NIST AI RMF calls for context-specific metrics, rigorous testing, uncertainty documentation, independent review, representative evaluation, validity/reliability evidence, and limits on generalizability: https://airc.nist.gov/airmf-resources/airmf/5-sec-core/ .
