# Serviceability and obtainability

Serviceability is a policy-defined subset of the total universe. Require a supplied serviceable count, same unit/source semantics, evidence ID, policy ID, and receipt ID. It must not exceed the total count.

Obtainability is a scenario, not a forecast. Require an exact fraction within the closed unit interval, explicit horizon, policy ID, receipt ID, and evidence ID. Apply it only to a valid serviceable value and label the output `SCENARIO_NOT_FORECAST`.

Do not invent competition, geography, product-fit, buyer-concentration, market-share, capacity, or sales-win-rate factors. A historical win rate among pursued deals does not establish obtainable share of an external population.
