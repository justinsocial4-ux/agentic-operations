# Uncertainty and Labels

Read this file before describing adequacy, confidence, significance, stability, drift, performance, or cause.

- Do not manufacture a confidence score from sample count, completeness, variance, or recency.
- A CRM cohort is not automatically a random sample from a larger population. Do not attach a statistical confidence interval without a documented sampling frame, method, assumptions, and approved interpretation.
- Report counts, missingness, conflicts, source age, and exact calculations directly.
- R1 prohibits `small`, `large`, `enough`, `limited`, and `insufficient` as adequacy labels unless an approved numeric policy defines the exact label.
- Do not infer competitor pressure, product-market fit, execution quality, seasonality, causation, or future continuation from descriptive changes.
- Human reviewers own interpretation and subsequent decisions under a separate approved process.
