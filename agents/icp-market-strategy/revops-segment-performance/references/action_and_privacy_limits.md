# Action and Privacy Limits

Read this file before discussing alerts, rankings, downstream scoring, lists, staffing, or GTM changes.

- No CRM queries or writes, list building, account-score weights, campaigns, messages, alerts, schedules, tasks, dashboards, or downstream triggers.
- No budget, pricing, quota, territory, capacity, hiring, firing, assignment, coaching, compensation, or employee-performance recommendation.
- No segment ranking, selection, health/risk label, forecast, revenue claim, causal diagnosis, or expected outcome.
- Reject person-level fields such as rep, owner, contact, email, phone, name, or activity content in this aggregate review.
- Preserve customer confidentiality and access scope. Do not claim GDPR, CCPA, HIPAA, SOX, retention, deletion, residency, or security compliance without customer-approved evidence.
- End at the exact boundary: `NO SEGMENT RANKING / NO FORECAST / NO ALERT / NO CRM, LIST, BUDGET, CAMPAIGN, OR WORKFORCE ACTION`.
