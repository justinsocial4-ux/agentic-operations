# Scope and Policy

Read this file before accepting the requested decision or review scope.

- The supported job is a read-only calculation from supplied evidence, not market discovery or strategy.
- Freeze the policy ID/version, purpose, cutoff, source schema, outcome mapping, opportunity identity/revision rule, segment definition, membership mode, periods, metric bases, comparison rule, roles, privacy/workforce policy, correction path, and prohibited uses.
- Require named owner, reviewer, and approver roles. `APPROVAL_REQUIRED` is not permission to act.
- Reject hidden assumptions, benchmark substitution, stale-cache substitution, and requests to rank, forecast, alert, allocate, or write.
- Create a new review version for corrections or new evidence; never silently mutate a frozen receipt.
