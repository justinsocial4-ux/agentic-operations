# Validation And Outcomes

A policy score can order records without being a predictive model. Require a model card before predictive language: declared construct and outcome, population, eligibility, feature provenance, missingness, time split, baseline, discrimination, calibration, uncertainty, subgroup performance, drift, limitations, version, owner, and approval.

Validate selection prospectively. Freeze policy/version, candidate cohort, cutoff, capacity, treatment, outcome window, and analysis plan before outcomes mature. Use a design that separates selection effects from campaign, seller effort, segment, timing, and treatment intensity.

Tier acceptance and seller intuition measure usability, not truth. Tier-to-tier conversion comparisons are confounded when tiers receive different treatment. Report descriptive outcomes as observational unless an approved randomized or credible quasi-experimental design supports incrementality.

Do not claim recovered pipeline, revenue lift, ROI, forecast improvement, time savings, or causal benefit without the matching evidence and amount basis.
