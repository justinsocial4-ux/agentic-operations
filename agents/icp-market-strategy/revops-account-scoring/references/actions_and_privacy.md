# Actions And Privacy Boundary

Minimize company/contact/activity data to the approved purpose. Associated-contact activity can contain personal data even when output is account-level. Require approved access, aggregation, retention, deletion, and downstream-use policies; send legal applicability questions to the customer's privacy/legal owner.

Remain read-only. Do not create/update score properties, lists, campaigns, tasks, territories, forecasts, budgets, routing, suppression, sequences, messages, or enrichment requests.

A proposed action must name exact records, policy/version, validation receipt, channel/system, expected decision, owner, approver, approval state, and reversal path. Analysis approval is not action approval. End outputs with `NO CRM WRITE / NO LIST / NO ROUTING / NO CAMPAIGN OR OUTREACH ACTION`.
