# Engagement Evidence Contract

For every activity type, define actor/direction, event ID, source, timestamp/timezone, associated contact/account rule, deduplication, bot/privacy-filter status, window, and points. Separate customer actions, seller actions, automated/system events, privacy/scanner events, and unknowns.

Email sends, tasks, page loads, ad impressions, and seller calls prove activity occurred—not buyer engagement or intent. Opens can be generated/hidden by privacy tools, scanners, clients, or self-opens. Replies and meetings still require identity, direction, outcome, and association evidence.

For company scores built from contacts, define eligible associated contacts and aggregation (`sum`, `count distinct`, `any`, etc.). Never default to maximum contact activity or assume one contact represents the account.

Keep engagement points separate from fit points even if the customer also approves a combined score.
