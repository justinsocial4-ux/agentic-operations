# Platform And Research Boundaries

- [HubSpot scoring overview](https://knowledge.hubspot.com/scoring/understand-the-lead-scoring-tool) documents configurable criteria, points, group/overall limits, association rules, and thresholds. These are customer configuration, not universal weights.
- [HubSpot score builder](https://knowledge.hubspot.com/scoring/build-lead-scores) separates company fit, engagement, and combined scores and lets customers set inclusion lists and thresholds.
- [HubSpot bot filtering](https://knowledge.hubspot.com/marketing-email/understand-bot-filtering-in-marketing-email-analytics) documents privacy filters and corporate scanners in email activity.
- [HubSpot email tracking limits](https://knowledge.hubspot.com/connected-email/understand-hubspot-sales-email-open-and-click-tracking) documents missing pixels, inferred opens, forwarding uncertainty, self-opens, and consent considerations.
- [Salesforce Einstein lead scoring](https://help.salesforce.com/s/articleView?id=sf.einstein_sales_els_how_it_works.htm&language=en_US&type=5) documents org/segment-specific models, omitted fields, global fallback, refreshes, and score factors.
- [NIST AI RMF Playbook](https://airc.nist.gov/docs/AI_RMF_Playbook.pdf) calls for validity checks including whether a system measures the intended construct.

Do not repeat the legacy 50/25/25 weights, 75/50 tiers, missing-value defaults, decay windows, interaction saturation, confidence formula, sample gates, enrichment uplift, runtime/API limits, accuracy, productivity, pipeline, conversion, velocity, win-rate, revenue, ROI, or compliance claims as facts.
