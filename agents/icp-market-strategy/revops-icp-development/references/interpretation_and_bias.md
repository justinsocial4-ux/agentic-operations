# Interpretation and Bias

Historical closed deals are selected by prior marketing, lists, qualification, routing, sales capacity, seller behavior, pricing, product, territory, and data capture. Segment differences may reflect these processes rather than customer fit.

Use `OBSERVED_SEGMENT_EVIDENCE`, `PROSPECTIVE_HYPOTHESIS`, `UNKNOWN`, and `CONFLICT`. Do not say causal fit, demand, intent, future probability, expected revenue, statistically significant, or drift without the required design.
