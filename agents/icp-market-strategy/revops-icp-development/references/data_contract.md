# Data Contract

Require pseudonymous deal/account IDs, frozen snapshot/cutoff, eligibility, outcome and maturity, close/created timestamps, pipeline/product/channel/owner cohort fields, firmographic raw values and mapping versions, amount basis/currency, and source provenance.

Exclude contact PII. Preserve duplicate/conflict/unmatched/excluded counts. Keep missing firmographics as a visible bucket. Do not reconstruct prior industry, revenue, employee count, territory, or outcome from current records.
