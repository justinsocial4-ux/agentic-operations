# Research and Claims

The legacy IMS-01 research has no completed independent QA audit. Its universal sample gates, 1.5× signal rule, confidence/drift formulas, data thresholds, vendor costs, labor, decay, and outcome claims are not approved decision evidence.

Use customer-controlled frozen CRM/warehouse evidence and a preapproved analysis plan. Platform documentation can establish field/history mechanics, not a universal ICP or causal segment effect.
