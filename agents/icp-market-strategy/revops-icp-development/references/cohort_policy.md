# Cohort Policy

Record policy ID/version, cutoff, eligible opportunity population, outcome states, maturity lag, analysis window, pipeline/product/channel/geography, account hierarchy, deduplication unit, exclusions, and comparison rules.

Wins alone cannot define ICP. Preserve lost, other, unresolved, missing, and excluded records. Comparable cohorts need identical policies/mappings and equivalent maturity; otherwise return `INCOMPARABLE_COHORTS`.
