# Research And Platform Boundaries

Current primary sources support evidence caution, not universal sales conclusions:

- [Gong: How Gong identifies speakers](https://help.gong.io/docs/how-gong-identifies-speakers) explains that identification varies by recording type; shared rooms, brief speakers, mono calls, and name correction create limitations. Do not treat diarization as verified identity without an approved mapping.
- [Gong: Calls and conversations tables](https://help.gong.io/docs/calls-and-conversations-tables) documents transcript JSON with speaker IDs and sentence start/end times. These support locators and exact time arithmetic.
- [Gong: View a call transcript](https://help.gong.io/docs/view-a-call-transcript) describes synced transcript/audio and manual vocabulary improvements. It does not establish a universal accuracy rate.
- [Gong: Download a call or snippet](https://help.gong.io/docs/download-a-call-or-snippet) warns that transcripts may contain sensitive business or personal information and ties downloads to permissions.
- [Zoom: Automated captions](https://support.zoom.com/hc/en/article?from=1&id=zm_kb&onlycontent=1&sysparm_article=KB0062813) says accuracy varies with background noise, volume, voice clarity, dialect, and lexicon.
- [Zoom: Recording notifications](https://support.zoom.com/hc/en/article?id=zm_kb&sysparm_article=KB0068228) documents participant notifications and configurable consent prompts. Platform behavior is not a universal legal conclusion.
- [NIST AI RMF Core](https://airc.nist.gov/airmf-resources/airmf/5-sec-core/) calls for documented scope, construct validation, provenance, human oversight, and risk controls.
- [NIST AI RMF Appendix C](https://airc.nist.gov/airmf-resources/airmf/appendices/app-c-ai-risk-management-and-human-ai-interaction/) warns that quantifying complex human phenomena can remove necessary context.
- [EEOC AI and Algorithmic Fairness initiative](https://www.eeoc.gov/newsroom/eeoc-launches-initiative-artificial-intelligence-and-algorithmic-fairness) states that employment laws still apply when AI informs employment decisions.
- [EU AI Act, Regulation 2024/1689](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX%3A32024R1689) includes restrictions on certain workplace emotion-inference systems. Send applicability questions to legal counsel; this skill avoids emotion inference entirely.

Do not repeat the legacy 90–95% transcription accuracy, 95% emotion accuracy, ideal talk/listen range, fixed confidence thresholds, coaching lift, time savings, win-rate lift, forecast improvement, API limits, retention periods, or legal conclusions as facts. Verify current primary documentation and customer policy for every platform-specific or regulated claim.
