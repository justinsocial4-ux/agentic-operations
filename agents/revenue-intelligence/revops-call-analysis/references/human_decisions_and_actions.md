# Human Decisions And Action Boundary

## Allowed output

Produce a read-only evidence review for a named, authorized reviewer. It may include exact quotes, approved tags, descriptive metrics, unknowns, contradictions, and a preview of a proposed downstream use.

## Prohibited automation

Do not:

- score, rank, reward, penalize, promote, discipline, terminate, or place a person on a performance plan;
- diagnose coaching needs or claim a person is coachable, effective, weak, dishonest, disengaged, or emotional;
- change deal stage, risk, forecast, amount, close date, contact role, or next step;
- create/update CRM records, tasks, fields, notes, objects, dashboards, or labels;
- send email/chat/Slack/Teams messages, share recordings/snippets, schedule meetings, or trigger outreach;
- change recording, access, redaction, retention, or deletion settings.

## Separate approval

A proposed action must name the exact action, system/channel, target record/audience, evidence receipt, policy owner, human approver, approval state, and reversal path. Approval for analysis is not approval for action.

Employment or worker-monitoring use requires the customer's HR/legal/privacy governance and independent validation. Deal decisions require the accountable sales/revenue owner. The skill itself remains read-only even after approval; execution belongs in a separately controlled workflow.

End every output with:

`NO CRM WRITE / NO TASK / NO MESSAGE / NO EMPLOYMENT OR DEAL DECISION`
