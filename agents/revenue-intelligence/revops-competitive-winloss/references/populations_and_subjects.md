# Populations and Subjects

Declare frozen pseudonymous opportunity and competitor populations with exact counts and review-window boundaries. Each approved lane declares whether it observes opportunities or competitors and must provide one complete source population equal to that declaration.

Do not silently remove subjects with no observation, missing codes, conflicting records, or zero events. Reject duplicate, extra, unknown, or wrong-subject IDs. A complete source population permits `NO_EVENT_RECORDED`; an incomplete population cannot prove absence.
