# Scope and Policy

Accept only the approved `competitive_win_loss_evidence_review` purpose. The effective policy must identify its owner, human reviewer, cutoff, timezone, correction path, prohibited uses, calculation policy, exact source/schema/code-map bindings, and any numeric observation rules.

Reject expired, future-effective, ambiguous, or partially bound policies. A corrected policy or source record starts a new frozen review. Human review does not authorize an alert, recommendation, write, or other external action.
