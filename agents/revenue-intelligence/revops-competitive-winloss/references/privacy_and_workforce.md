# Privacy and Workforce Boundary

Use pseudonymous opportunity, competitor, source, observation, policy, and role IDs. Reject customer/account names, company domains, URLs, emails, phones, addresses, notes, transcripts, interview quotes, review text, posts, job titles, protected traits, precise worker identity, and free text.

Do not calculate or expose seller, manager, interviewer, buyer, or annotator performance. Do not recommend coaching, reassignment, discipline, compensation, quota, surveillance, or individual action. Minimize rendered data to the source-bound codes and receipts required for the approved review.
